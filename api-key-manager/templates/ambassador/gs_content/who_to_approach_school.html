Your main target

Focus on owners and administrators of private and independent schools — nursery, primary, or secondary. Government schools are not a fit; PhiXtra School is built for privately-run schools that manage their own fees and admissions.

🏫 Private/Independent Schools
The best question to ask

Ask this when you speak to a school proprietor or administrator. The answer tells you right away if they need PhiXtra School:

"How do parents currently reach you about fees or their child's attendance — and how many messages or calls does that take up in a day?"
If they say "it's constant, mostly phone calls and WhatsApp" — they need PhiXtra.
If they say the front office struggles to keep up — they really need PhiXtra.
If they say "we don't have a system for this" — you have a sale.
Signs of a good school to approach
✅
Parents call or WhatsApp the school office daily about fees, attendance, or pickup times
✅
Fee collection and reminders are still tracked manually — spreadsheets, notebooks, or memory
✅
The school has a WhatsApp number parents already use, even informally
✅
Growing student roll (50+ students) — big enough that manual parent communication is becoming a strain
✅
The proprietor or administrator is open to new tools — already uses a school management app, POS, or similar software
Where to find them
🏫
Private school clusters — most towns have a concentration of independent nursery/primary/secondary schools in the same neighbourhood
🤝
Private school owners' associations — state or local chapters of independent school proprietor associations
📱
Facebook and Instagram — school pages posting admission announcements, resumption dates, or events
👥
School-owner WhatsApp groups — proprietors comparing notes on running their schools
