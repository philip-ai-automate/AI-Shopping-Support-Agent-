What is PhiXtra School?

PhiXtra School is a management portal for private and independent schools, built around an AI assistant that answers parents on WhatsApp automatically. Instead of the front office fielding endless calls and messages about fees, attendance, and closing times, parents message the school's WhatsApp number and the AI replies instantly — day or night.

🎓
Answers parent questions — fee balances, attendance, and general school Q&A, answered from the school's own data and documents, not generic guesses.
📋
Runs attendance & fees — staff mark attendance and record fee payments in the portal; parents can be notified automatically.
📢
Sends broadcasts — absence alerts and fee reminders go out to parents over WhatsApp using approved message templates.
👩‍🏫
Scoped staff access — teachers only see their own class; admins and bursars see the full school.
🔀
Hands over to staff when needed — if a parent's question needs a human, staff can see the conversation and step in.
