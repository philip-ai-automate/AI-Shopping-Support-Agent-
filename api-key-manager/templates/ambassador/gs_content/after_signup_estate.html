Why you need to follow up

If an agency signs up but never connects WhatsApp or adds listings, they will not activate a paid subscription — and you will not earn anything. A short follow-up from you makes a big difference.

Things to do after they sign up
1
Check they got the welcome email
Tell them to check their spam folder if they do not see it. The email has their login link.
2
Ask them to log in at home.phixtra.com
New accounts start on a Pro plan trial, so they have full access to explore before choosing a plan.
3
Ask them to add their property listings
The AI needs listings to answer buyer questions. Listings are added one at a time under "Listings → New".
4
Help them connect their WhatsApp number
This is the step that activates the AI. Point them to the Client Requirements list if they haven't got the SIM/Meta Business Account ready yet.
5
Check in after 2–3 days, then again before the trial ends
Ask if the AI is working well and leads are being assigned properly. Then follow up again before the Pro plan trial runs out — that conversion to a paid plan is what actually earns you commission, so don't let it lapse silently.

Connect your WhatsApp number (I can help with this) Once your number is connected, the AI will start answering buyer questions for you. Let me know if you need any help — I am here!
