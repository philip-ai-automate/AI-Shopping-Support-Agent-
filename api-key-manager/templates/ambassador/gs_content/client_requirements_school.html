What to tell the school

Registering for PhiXtra School is much simpler than Portal — it's a self-serve web sign-up with no SIM card, Meta Business Account, or WhatsApp linking required upfront. Just two things need to be ready.

  1. Registration is done by one person — usually the proprietor or principal — who becomes the account admin.
  2. They'll need a working email address to register and log in.
💡 They can invite teachers and other staff into the portal after registering, with access scoped to their own class or role.
  1. School name, school type (nursery, primary, or secondary), and state.
  2. An approximate student count — this determines which pricing plan fits them (see Common Questions for plan details).
💡 Registration takes about 5 minutes — there's no waiting period or manual approval before they can log in and start setting up classes.
  1. Parent WhatsApp notifications (absence alerts, fee reminders) are a separate step after registration — not a requirement to sign up.
  2. Let them know the PhiXtra team helps connect the WhatsApp number when they're ready.
Tip: Because there's so little to prepare, most schools can register and start adding classes the same day you speak to them.
