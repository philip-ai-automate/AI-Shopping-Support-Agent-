How does PhiXtra Real Estate actually work?
The most common first question
▼

PhiXtra Real Estate connects to your agency's WhatsApp number. When a buyer messages asking about a property, the AI replies instantly using your actual listings — never a property you haven't listed.

1
You connect your WhatsApp
Your agency's WhatsApp number is linked using the official Meta Business API — the same number your buyers already message.
2
You add your listings
Each property is added with its price, location, type, and details. The AI only recommends what's actually listed.
3
The AI qualifies buyers
It naturally collects budget, preferred location, property type, and timeline in conversation — then hands off to a human agent the moment the buyer is serious.
4
Your agents stay in control
The AI never quotes a final price as fixed and never processes payment — it always says "subject to agent confirmation" and hands over for anything serious.
