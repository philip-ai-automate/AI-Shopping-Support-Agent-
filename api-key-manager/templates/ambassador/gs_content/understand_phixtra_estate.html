What is PhiXtra Real Estate?

PhiXtra Real Estate is an AI property assistant that runs on a real estate agency's WhatsApp line. When a buyer messages asking about a property — price, location, bedrooms, availability — the AI replies automatically, qualifies the buyer, and hands off to a human agent the moment they're seriously interested.

🏠
Knows the agency's listings — the AI only recommends properties the agency has actually listed, and never fabricates details it doesn't have.
🇳🇬
Speaks Nigerian property — understands C of O, Governor's Consent, Survey Plan, Excision, agency and legal fees, and replies in Pidgin if the buyer does.
🧠
Qualifies buyers naturally — budget, preferred location, property type, bedrooms, and timeline, gathered through conversation, not a form.
🤝
Hands over at the right moment — the moment a buyer states a budget, asks to inspect, or says they're interested, a human agent takes over. It never quotes a final price or processes payment itself.
📊
The agency stays in control — staff can log in any time to see every conversation, manage listings, and assign leads to agents.
