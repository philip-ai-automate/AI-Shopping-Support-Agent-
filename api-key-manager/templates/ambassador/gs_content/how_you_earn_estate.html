Monthly commission

You earn 30% of every payment from agencies that sign up through your own referral link (20% for clients PhiXtra assigns to you) — monthly or annual, whichever they choose. This is paid automatically — you do not need to ask or send an invoice.

Level Active Agencies Your Cut How Long
Standard 1 – 9 30% 12 months
Elite 10 – 19 30% 24 months
Master 🏆 20+ 30% Forever
Your level goes up as more of your referred agencies become paying subscribers. The more agencies you bring in, the longer you keep earning from each one.
