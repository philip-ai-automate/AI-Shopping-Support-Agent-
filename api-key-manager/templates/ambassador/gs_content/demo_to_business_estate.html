Why show a demo first?

Telling an agency about the AI is good. Showing them the real dashboard and how a buyer conversation flows is better. Most agency owners get convinced once they see the actual screens.

The demo account

There's a shared live Demo Agency account with a "Sandbox" page that simulates a live buyer chat with the Property Assistant AI — that's your self-serve tool for showing a prospect the AI in action.

⚠️
Check before you demo the chat sandbox. It currently has no property listings seeded, so a live sandbox chat may come back empty. Confirm with your PhiXtra contact whether demo listings have been added before relying on it in front of a prospect. If it isn't ready, lead with the PDF/pitch instead and offer to arrange a proper demo — see below.
🙅
Don't log in and walk a client through the full dashboard yourself. If a prospect wants to see the listings, staff, and lead screens, arrange it with a Presale Engineer instead of demoing it yourself.
How to run the demo
1
Open the Sandbox chat in front of the agency owner
Only if you've confirmed listings are seeded (see warning above). Do this while they are watching.
2
Explain how the AI qualifies a buyer
Walk through how a buyer's WhatsApp question gets answered, how the AI collects budget/location/timeline, and how it hands off to a human agent once the buyer is serious.
3
Say this to close
"This is what your buyers get once your listings are connected. Want me to help you register right now?" If they want to see the full dashboard and listings screens first, offer to arrange that with a Presale Engineer rather than showing it yourself.
After the demo
👉
