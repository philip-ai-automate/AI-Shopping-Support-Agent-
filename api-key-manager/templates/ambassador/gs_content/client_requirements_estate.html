What to tell the agency

Registering for PhiXtra Real Estate is quick and self-serve — no requirements to sign up. But before the AI can start answering buyers on WhatsApp, the agency needs three things ready. Share this list once they agree to register.

  1. Get a brand new SIM card — or any number that has never been used on WhatsApp before. This number is only for the agency.
  2. If the number is already on WhatsApp, they must delete that WhatsApp account first before continuing.
  3. Keep the SIM active — they will need a one-time code (OTP) on that number during setup.
  4. Give the number to PhiXtra — the team will connect it to WhatsApp Business for them.
💡 MTN, Glo, Airtel, and 9mobile SIMs all work fine.
  1. Go to business.facebook.com on a computer and click "Create Account".
  2. Enter the agency name, owner's name, and a business email address. Click Submit.
  3. Check that email inbox — click the confirmation link Meta sends.
  4. Find the Meta Business ID: go to Settings → Business Info — it is a long number near the top.
💡 They need a personal Facebook account to do this. It is free to create one.
  1. Log in to business.facebook.com and go to Settings → Business Assets → WhatsApp Accounts.
  2. Click "Add" and type in the agency's phone number.
  3. Meta will send a one-time code (OTP) to that number — enter it to confirm.
  4. Share the phone number and Meta Business ID with PhiXtra to finish the connection.
💡 Meta can take 1–2 days to verify. This is normal — PhiXtra support will help them through it.
  1. For each property: address/area, price, property type, bedrooms (if residential), and photos.
  2. Nigerian-specific details help the AI answer well — land title type (C of O, Governor's Consent, Excision), and whether the price is negotiable or fixed.
  3. Listings are added one by one under "Listings → New" after registering — there's no bulk spreadsheet import yet.
💡 The more detail in each listing, the better the AI can answer buyer questions — but it will never quote a final price as fixed, always "subject to agent confirmation."
Tip: Send this list to the agency owner on WhatsApp after they agree to sign up. The sooner they get these ready, the sooner they go live — and the sooner you start earning.
