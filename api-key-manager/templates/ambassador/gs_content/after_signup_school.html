Why you need to follow up

If a school registers but never sets up their classes and students, they will not activate a paid plan — and you will not earn anything. A short follow-up from you makes a big difference.

Things to do after they sign up
1
Check they got the welcome email
Tell them to check their spam folder if they do not see it. The email has their login link.
2
Ask them to log in at school.phixtra.com
From there, they add their classes and student roster — this is what activates the portal for their staff and parents.
3
Ask them to invite teachers and staff
Each teacher can be scoped to their own class; admins and bursars see the full school.
4
Encourage them to connect WhatsApp for parent notifications
This unlocks automatic absence alerts and fee reminders — the feature that impresses parents most.
5
Check in after a week, then nudge toward a paid plan
Ask if the portal is working well for their staff. Commission only fires once they move off the Free plan, so once they're settled in, encourage them to upgrade — point them to your PhiXtra contact for anything you can't answer.
Send them this on WhatsApp after they sign up
Copy & send this
Hi [Name], welcome to PhiXtra School! 🎉 Here is how to get started: 1. Log in at school.phixtra.com 2. Add your classes and student roster 3. Invite your teachers and staff Once you're ready, let me know and I'll help you connect WhatsApp for parent notifications. Let me know if you need anything — I'm here!
