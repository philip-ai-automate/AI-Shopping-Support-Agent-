Your main target

Focus on real estate agencies and agents in Nigeria who list and sell or let properties — from small independent agents to multi-agent agencies.

🏠 Real Estate Agencies
🧑‍💼 Independent Agents

Agencies juggling more than a handful of active listings get the most value — the AI handles the repetitive "is it available / how much / where is it" questions so agents can focus on serious buyers.

The best question to ask

Ask this when you speak to an agency owner or agent. The answer tells you right away if they need PhiXtra Real Estate:

"How many WhatsApp messages or calls do you get a day asking if a property is still available or how much it costs?"
If they say it's constant across several listings — they need PhiXtra.
If they say agents waste time repeating the same answers — they really need PhiXtra.
If they say "we're losing track of who's serious" — you have a sale.
Signs of a good agency to approach
✅
They have multiple active listings advertised on WhatsApp, Instagram, or Facebook
✅
Buyers WhatsApp or call constantly asking about price, location, or availability
✅
They have more than one agent, and leads sometimes fall through the cracks
✅
They already pay for property listing sites or other business tools
Where to find them
🏢
Real estate agency offices — most cities have clusters of agency offices in commercial districts
🤝
Estate surveyors' and agents' associations — state chapters of professional real estate bodies
📱
Instagram and Facebook — accounts posting property listings with a WhatsApp number in their bio
👥
Real estate WhatsApp groups — agents and landlords sharing listings and referrals
