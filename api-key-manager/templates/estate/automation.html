Automatic next actions
Every open lead always has one next action. With this on, KeyLand fills it in when something happens, so nobody has to remember.
  • New lead: reply and find out what they want, within 2 hours
  • AI hands a buyer over: call them, within 1 hour
  • Buyer becomes Ready: call to fix the site inspection or viewing, same day
  • Inspection or viewing booked: confirm it and send directions, the day before
  • Visit date passed: ask for their decision, 2 days after
  • Plot linked to the lead: collect the deposit, within 3 days
  • Deposit paid: allocate the plot and send the allocation letter, within 2 days
KeyLand never replaces an action a person set. If something new happens, the lead shows a suggestion beside it instead. With this off, agents set every next action themselves.
Automatic WhatsApp follow-ups

A WhatsApp rule to know. Within 24 hours of the buyer's last message, you can send any wording. After that, WhatsApp only delivers wording Meta has approved first. So a follow-up with a wait of 24 hours or more is sent to Meta when you save with follow-ups on, and goes out only once it's approved (usually minutes, sometimes a day). Meta charges your WhatsApp account for these approved messages; the ones under 24 hours have no template charge.

Alerts to the agent and the managers
When a lead is slipping, the agent and the managers (you and every admin) are told at the same moment, by email, WhatsApp and phone notification. With this off, the Needs attention panel still shows everything and nobody is messaged.
  • AI hands a buyer over: straight away, then one reminder if nobody has acted after the chat limit below. This replaces the email to your main address.
  • Ready buyer not contacted: after the limit below, then one reminder after the same time again.
  • Assigned lead: the agent and the managers. Unassigned: every agent with alerts on, plus the managers.
  • Only real action stops an alert: logging a call or contact, marking the next action done, marking the hand-over handled, or closing the lead. Opening the lead doesn't count.
  • Overdue actions and leads with no next action aren't alerted one by one. They're in the panel and the morning summary.
