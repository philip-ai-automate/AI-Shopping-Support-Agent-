How it works
  1. A buyer accepts a quotation (online, by reply, or you mark the deal Won).
  2. On the deal or the quotation request, press Create QuickBooks invoice.
  3. PhiXtra makes the invoice in QuickBooks with the quoted lines and prices, for the buyer's QuickBooks customer (found by email or name, or made new). QuickBooks does not email it.
  4. Your accountant checks it and presses Send in QuickBooks. QuickBooks chases payment as usual.
  5. The deal shows the invoice: not sent yet, awaiting payment, paid or overdue.
