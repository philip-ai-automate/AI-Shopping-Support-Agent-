MP4 (H.264) works everywhere. Other videos, such as iPhone MOV files, are converted automatically when you upload them. Up to 300 MB.

One design for every network?

Use 1:1 square, 1080 × 1080. It shows uncropped on Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn and Threads, and is only lightly trimmed on X.

File tips

  • PNG for designs with text or logos (sharpest)
  • JPG for photos (smaller files)
  • At least 1080 pixels wide, or it looks blurry
  • Keep words away from the outer edge, in case a network trims it
