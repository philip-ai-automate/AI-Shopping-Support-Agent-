How it works
  • You sign in with Microsoft once. PhiXtra can only read your mail. It never sends, moves or deletes anything.
  • Every 2 minutes it looks for replies from people your business emailed in the last 60 days.
  • A reply shows on that person's lead with its first few lines and an "Open in Outlook" link, moves a New lead to Contacted, and stops any email sequence they're in.
  • All other mail is skipped and never stored. Out-of-office replies and bounced emails are ignored.
  • When you first connect, replies from the last 14 days are picked up too.
  • Works with Microsoft 365 work accounts. "They replied" on the lead still works for replies that come another way.
