How it works
  1. A buyer accepts a quotation (online, by reply, or you mark the deal Won).
  2. On the deal or the quotation request, press Create Sage invoice.
  3. PhiXtra makes a draft invoice in Sage with the quoted lines and prices, for the buyer's Sage contact (found by email or name, or made new).
  4. Your accountant checks it and releases it in Sage. Sage sends it and chases payment as usual.
  5. PhiXtra checks the invoice every hour: the deal shows awaiting payment, paid or overdue.
