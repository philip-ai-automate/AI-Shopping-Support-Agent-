Your number stays on your WhatsApp Business app. The same chats also show in PhiXtra, where your team and AI Agent can answer them.

Have ready
What to do
  1. In PhiXtraPress Connect WhatsApp, then Connect and keep my app.
  2. In the Meta windowLog in with Facebook and follow it to the end.
  3. On your phoneWhatsApp sends a message to your Business app. Tap Connect, then Share chat history.
  4. In PhiXtraYour number shows as Connected. That's it.
Leave "Copy my WhatsApp contacts" ticked if you want your contacts in PhiXtra. WhatsApp lets us copy them only once.

PhiXtra connects to the WhatsApp Business app, so first move your number to it. It's free, and your chats come with you.

Have ready
What to do
  1. On your phoneInstall WhatsApp Business from the App Store or Google Play.
  2. On your phoneOpen it and choose your number. Your chats move across from WhatsApp.
  3. In PhiXtraPress Connect WhatsApp, then Connect and keep my app, and follow the Meta window.
  4. On your phoneTap Connect, then Share chat history.

Customers message this number, and your team and AI Agent answer in PhiXtra. The number then works only through PhiXtra, not in a WhatsApp app on a phone.

Have ready
What to do
  1. In PhiXtraPress Connect WhatsApp. Under "Use a new number", press Connect with WhatsApp.
  2. In the Meta windowLog in with Facebook and follow it.
  3. On the phoneMeta sends a code by text or call. Type it in the Meta window.
  4. In PhiXtraYour number shows as Connected.
{# Page 2 — Products #}

So your AI Agent can answer "how much?" and "do you have…?" questions. Until then, it passes them to your team.

XLS Product templateOnly Name and Price are needed. Delete our 3 example rows and add yours.
⬇ Excel⬇ CSV
What to do
  1. In ExcelFill in the template, or use your own list. One row per product or service.
  2. In PhiXtraPress Upload a list and choose the file.
  3. In PhiXtraChoose which column is the name and which is the price, then save.
Only a few? Add them one by one in My Products & Services. Sell services? Press I sell services; a name and a price is enough. No stock column means always available.
{# Page 3 — Trial #}

Your AI Agent answers your WhatsApp customers in their language, day and night, for 14 days. Free, and no card needed.

Best done first
What happens
  1. Press Start free trial. It starts straight away.
  2. Your AI Agent answers customers on WhatsApp.
  3. When a person is needed, it hands the chat to your team in the Inbox.
After 14 days, choose a plan to keep the AI Agent answering. Your settings are kept, and your team can still answer from the Inbox.
