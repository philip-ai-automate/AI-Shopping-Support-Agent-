How you make quotations, how fast requests should be answered, and who is told.

How do you make quotations?

"Add to quote" on your website

WordPress / WooCommerce: the PhiXtra plugin adds an "Add to quote" button on every product page. Any other website: the PhiXtra chat button gets a "Request a quote" option. Buyers send their list with their name and email; no account needed. An estimate is always called an estimate: your quotation is the only price that counts. After you switch it on or off, a WordPress shop shows the change within 10 minutes.

Follow-ups after a quote

When you click Quote sent, the deal moves to Proposal sent. Messages to buyers who go quiet are set in Pipeline Settings › Automatic follow-ups (rule: "When a deal moves into a stage and there's no reply", stage Proposal sent). The salesperson is also reminded 2 days before a quote expires, and asked to extend or close it when it does.
