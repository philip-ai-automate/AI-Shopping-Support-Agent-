Quotation settings

How you make quotations, how fast requests should be answered, and who is told.

How do you make quotations?

"Add to quote" on your website

WordPress / WooCommerce: the PhiXtra plugin adds an "Add to quote" button on every product page. Any other website: the PhiXtra chat button gets a "Request a quote" option. Buyers send their list with their name and email; no account needed. An estimate is always called an estimate: your quotation is the only price that counts. After you switch it on or off, a WordPress shop shows the change within 10 minutes.

Online quotations

{% for k, w in [('online_link', 'Send a link so buyers can view the quotation online'), ('online_accept', 'Let buyers accept or decline online'), ('online_open_alert', 'Email the salesperson the first time a buyer opens a quotation')] %} {% endfor %}
The link goes in the email or WhatsApp message the quotation is sent with, and can go in follow-ups as {{ '{{' }}Quote Link{{ '}}' }}. It only opens that quotation, stops working when it expires, is replaced by a new version, or is Won or Lost, and is never kept by search engines. Accepting needs the buyer's name and a tick; the salesperson is told and can press Undo. If you make quotations in your own software, the link is made when you upload the PDF at Quote sent.

Quote follow-ups

Each one can also start one of your own automatic follow-ups (emails and WhatsApp messages). It stops when the buyer replies, your team gets in touch, or the quote is marked Won or Lost. In your messages you can use {{ '{{' }}Quote Number{{ '}}' }}, {{ '{{' }}Quote Value{{ '}}' }}, {{ '{{' }}Valid Until{{ '}}' }} and {{ '{{' }}Quote Link{{ '}}' }}. When "before a quote runs out" is off, the salesperson still gets an email that many days before. The day after a quote runs out, they're asked to extend it or close it as Lost.
