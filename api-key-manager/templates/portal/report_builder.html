Changes show straight away. "+ Only show … where" adds a condition (Field, Rule, Value, Add); remove one with its ×. "More options" has "And also split by" (for a stacked chart, heatmap or pivot table), "Count by date" (which date puts a record in the period) and Folder. Two tabs: "List" (every record; the blue "Columns (n)" button picks and orders the columns — tick what you need, drag to reorder, then the green "Use these columns" button; click a heading to sort) and "Chart and totals" (the chart, the totals and a totals table). The "Columns" button is also shown on the Chart tab and opens the list with the column chooser. "Show as" buttons: Bar, Pie, Line, Table, Big number, and "More ▾" for Donut, Horizontal bar, Stacked bar, Area, Bar + line, Funnel, Heatmap and Pivot. The chart you chose is highlighted. A greyed one says why when you point at it (most charts need a split; Stacked bar, Heatmap and Pivot need "And also split by"; Pie, Donut and Funnel need a number or a total, not an average). Example, pie chart of quotes won by each team member: Reports › All reports › + New report, pick Quotes, then "Who wins the most quotes?" or Start from blank: What to count = Total quote value, Which quotes = Won, Split it by = Team member, Show it as = Pie, Create report. Example, pivot table of quote value by person and month: split by Team member, More options › And also split by Month (requested on), Show as › More › Pivot. Click any number, slice, bar or pivot cell to see the records behind it in a side panel ("Export these (Excel)", "Open as a report"). "Save report" keeps it (the name is at the top of the page; Folder is under More options). "Save as new" keeps your own copy of someone else's report. "Export ▾" gives Excel, CSV or PDF. "Add to dashboard ▾" puts a saved report on a dashboard. "Share" (on a saved report you made): Who can open it = Only me, Chosen team members or roles, or Everyone in the business; What numbers they see = Follow each person's access (a sales rep who only sees their own deals sees only their own numbers) or Everyone sees the whole business. Tick "Others can edit it". Press "Save sharing". Only the person who saved a report can share or delete it ("Delete report" under the name).
