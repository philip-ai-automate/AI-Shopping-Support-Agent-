Before you connect: what Meta doesn't allow
Meta runs WhatsApp and can lock or ban a WhatsApp account that breaks its rules. That stops every WhatsApp message, in PhiXtra and in the WhatsApp app.
Pay-to-join or "earn money" schemes
Registration fees to start earning, pyramid or referral income, guaranteed returns.
Loans, crypto and gambling
Lending, crypto trading, betting and lotteries are restricted.
Banned products
Weapons, drugs, tobacco and vapes, adult items, live animals, fake goods.
Messages nobody asked for
Messaging people who never gave you their number, or many people blocking you.
Full lists: Meta's Commerce Policy and WhatsApp Business Policy.
Please tick this first, then press connect again.
