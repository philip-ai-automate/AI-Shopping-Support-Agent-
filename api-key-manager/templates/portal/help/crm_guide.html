1How the CRM fits together

CRM means "customer relationship management": one place that remembers every customer, what you've said to them, and where each sale has got to.

Open CRM in the side menu and you'll see seven pages:

All ContactsYour one address book: every person or business you deal with, with their phone number, WhatsApp number, email, status, labels, notes and history.
CompaniesThe businesses behind your contacts. Several contacts can belong to one company.
Pipeline OverviewA report of your open deals: how many, what they're worth, and how fast they move.
Pipeline BoardWhere you work your deals, moving each one stage by stage until it's won or closed.
SegmentsCRM's own named lists of contacts (for example "Lagos customers"). WhatsApp and Email campaigns have their own segments; see section 9.
LabelsShort markers such as "VIP" or "Follow-up" that you can put on contacts and deals.
Pipeline SettingsChange the names of your pipeline stages and of the Hot / Warm / Cold score words.

Contacts and deals are different things

This is the most important idea in the CRM:

  • A contact is a person or business. They stay in your CRM for as long as you keep them.
  • A deal (the portal also calls it a Sales Lead) is one chance to sell something to that contact. It starts, moves through stages, and ends as Won, Lost or Dropped.

One contact can have several deals over time: a first order in March, a repeat order in August. Every deal is joined to its contact, so a contact's page shows all their deals, and a deal's page links back to the contact. Closing a deal never deletes the contact.

How a customer moves through the CRM

Someone messages you, leaves details in your website chat, or you add or import them→Contact→Lead (Leads menu)→Qualify→Deal on the Pipeline Board→Won / Lost / Dropped
  1. People who message your WhatsApp number, and website chat visitors who leave a number or email, are added to All Contacts automatically. You can also add people by hand or import a spreadsheet. Adding a Sales Lead also adds its contact if they're new. How this works.
  2. When someone shows buying interest, a Lead is created. This happens automatically from campaign replies and the AI, or you press Create Sales Lead on a contact.
  3. Leads wait on the Leads page until someone decides: Qualify (a real sales chance) or Not a Fit. The Leads guide covers this step in full.
  4. A qualified lead appears on the Pipeline Board as a deal at the first stage. From here your team moves it forward stage by stage.

Why you might not see a button

  • A gold padlock 🔒 next to a menu item means your business's plan doesn't include that page. The owner can upgrade under Settings › My Plan.
  • A button is missing altogether means your role doesn't allow that action (for example, only some staff may delete contacts). Ask the person who manages your team. Section 12 lists every permission.

Type part of a name, a number (phone or WhatsApp) or an email in Search by name, number or email… and press Search.

Warnings while you type

The form checks your typing against what's already in the CRM and shows a yellow warning if:

  • "A contact with this number already exists". The warning names the existing contact.
  • "Similar contact(s) already exist, did you mean…". A close name, allowing for small typos.
  • "Similar company already exists" or "Similar label already exists". Pick the existing one instead of making a near-duplicate (for example "Bello Fabric" vs "Bello Fabrics").
CarefulIf either number or the email already belongs to a contact, saving updates that contact instead of making a second one, and you'll see "… was already in your contacts, so their details were updated". The name, contact person, notes, personalization and status you typed replace theirs, and fields you left blank blank them out. Numbers and email are only added where they were empty. If the warning appears, edit the existing contact instead.

  3. Press Import CSV, drag the file onto the box (or click to choose it), then press Import Contacts.
  4. A message tells you how many were imported, how many were already in your contacts (and updated), and how many were skipped.

Exporting contacts

Export CSV downloads a file called contacts.csv with these columns: phone_number, whatsapp_number, email, name, contact_person, notes, labels, added. It uses the same column names as the import, so an exported file can be edited and imported back.

It exports only what you're currently looking at. Search or filter first to export a smaller group (for example all Customers with an email).

Deleting a contact

Deleting can't be undone

A contact with open Sales Leads can't be deleted. The portal says, for example, "Tunde Bakare has 1 open Sales Lead. Close or delete those leads first, then delete the contact." Deals that are already Won, Lost or Dropped don't block it.

When you delete a contact, these are removed with it: their notes, their labels, their segment memberships and their consent (opt-out) history.

These are kept: their closed deals (with their own name and numbers, just no longer linked), their call log entries, and their WhatsApp conversation in the Inbox.

If you just want to stop contacting someone, set them to Inactive or opt them out (see Consent) instead of deleting.

Consent

This panel controls whether your marketing campaigns can reach this person. It does not stop you replying to them in a normal chat.

  • The top line says Subscribed to marketing or Opted out of marketing.
  • Opt out of all stops WhatsApp and Email campaigns to them in one go.
  • Each channel (WhatsApp and Email) has its own button. Click Subscribed to switch that channel off; click Opted out to switch it back on.
  • Under the buttons is a history of every change: which channel, whether they opted out or back in, why, and when. It records changes made by staff and ones the customer made themselves (for example clicking the unsubscribe link in an email, which opts them out of everything).
Respect opt-outsOnly switch someone back on if they have clearly asked to hear from you again. The history is kept so you can show when and how consent changed.

Segments

Lists every CRM Segment this person is in that you're allowed to see. Click a name to open the segment, or press ✕ to take them out of it (the contact itself is not deleted). Use the drop-down in this box to add them to another CRM Segment. WhatsApp and Email Segments are managed on their own pages (section 9).

Profile Notes vs notes on the timelineProfile Notes (in Edit Profile) is one piece of text that you overwrite. Use it for lasting facts ("Prefers WhatsApp, orders quarterly"). Add Note creates a new dated entry each time. Use it for things that happened.

A company is the business behind one or more contacts. Linking contacts to their company lets you see everyone you know at, say, Ikorodu Plastics, and the total value of that company's open deals.

  2. Type the Company name and, if you like, the Website. A warning appears if a similar company already exists.

Open the contact, press Edit Profile, choose the company in Company, and save. Any deal belonging to that contact is linked to the same company automatically.

A company's page

  • At the top: the name, website, number of People and the value of Open Deals.
  • Edit changes the name or website.
  • People at this company: each linked contact, their phone, and their deal stage (or "No deal"). Click a name to open them.
  • Company Activity & Notes: notes about the business as a whole, such as a meeting or an agreement. Type and press Add Note.
Good to knowCompanies can't be deleted at the moment. To remove a person from a company, edit the contact and set Company to "— No Company —".

5Merge Review

Appears from the yellow bar on All Contacts when there is something to check

The CRM joins a deal to its contact automatically when a number or the email matches (a local number like 0816… also matches the same number saved as 234816…). When two numbers are only almost the same (for example one is missing a digit), it doesn't guess. It puts them here for a person to decide.

Each item shows the WhatsApp contact on one side, the Sales Pipeline lead on the other, and why they look alike.

  • Confirm, Same Person links them together permanently.
  • Not the Same keeps them separate, and the pair won't be suggested again.
CarefulConfirming can't be undone. If you aren't sure, check both phone numbers with the customer first.

6Pipeline Overview

CRM › Pipeline Overview

A read-only report on your deals. Use it in a weekly sales meeting.

Choosing the time period

Press Last 7 days, Last 30 days (the default) or Last 90 days, or choose two dates and press Apply custom range. Reset to last 30 days goes back.

The period only changes the numbers about closed deals: Won, Lost, Dropped, Win Rate and Avg. Time to Close. Active Deals and Open Pipeline Value are always "right now".

The numbers

BoxMeaning
Active DealsDeals still open today.
Open Pipeline ValueThe total ₦ value of those open deals.
WonValue and number of deals won in the period.
Win RateWon ÷ (Won + Lost). Dropped deals are left out, because you chose not to pursue them.
Lost / DroppedHow many deals closed that way in the period.
Avg. Time to CloseOn average, how many days a won deal took from start to win.

The charts

  • Funnel, deals by stage: a bar per stage. The longer the bar, the more deals sit there right now, with their total value.
  • Deals by stage: the same as a table, adding the average deal size.
  • Avg. time in each stage: how many days deals usually wait before moving to the next stage. A long bar shows where deals get stuck. This uses all history, not just the chosen period.

Downloading the report

Next to Export: press 🧾 CSV, 📊 Excel or 📄 PDF. The file uses the period you've chosen.

Moving a deal to the next stage

  1. Click ⋮ on the deal, then Move to ….
  2. A box explains what the new stage means and asks for the details in the stage table.
  3. Fill them in and press Confirm.

Editing a deal

Edit Deal lets you correct the Customer / Business Name, Contact Person, Phone, Email, WhatsApp Number (if different from phone), Deal Value, Product / Service Interested In and Notes. It never changes the stage.

It also has three Sales Signals that raise the deal's score: Budget confirmed, Decision-maker engaged and Deadline / timeline stated. See Lead score.

History

Closing a deal as Lost or Dropped

  • Lost: you tried, but the customer chose someone else or said no. Reasons: Price too high, Customer chose a competitor, Budget unavailable, No longer interested, Other.
  • Dropped: you decided not to pursue it. Reasons: No response, Invalid lead, Wrong contact, Duplicate, Other.
  1. Click ⋮ and choose Mark as Lost or Mark as Dropped.
  2. Pick a reason (required). If you choose Other, type a short explanation.
  3. Press Confirm.

The deal leaves the active list and moves to Closed, Lost & Dropped at the bottom of Board view, showing the outcome, the stage it had reached, the reason and the date. Lost deals count against your Win Rate; Dropped deals don't.

Good to knowA closed deal can't be reopened. If the customer comes back, create a new Sales Lead from their contact page. Their earlier history is kept.

Every deal gets a score, built from six facts on its record. Click the score bubble on a card ("Why this score?") to see how it was worked out.

8A deal's page

Pipeline Board › ⋮ › Open Lead Page, or click a deal's name

One page per deal, with everything about it.

  • Header: customer name, product of interest, the current stage badge (or WON / LOST / DROPPED), and ✏️ Edit Lead.
  • Contact: the contact this deal belongs to. Click the name to open their profile. If the deal has no phone, WhatsApp number or email yet, it says "No contact yet: add a phone, WhatsApp or email", with an Add details → button; once you add one, the deal is joined to a contact.
  • Details: Contact Person, Phone, WhatsApp, Email, Source (WhatsApp, Facebook, Instagram or Manual), Campaign Source (the campaign that produced it, if any), Company, Created, Product / Service, Assigned Salesperson, Lead Score with its breakdown, and Deal Value.
  • Pipeline: a progress line showing stages done (✓), the current stage (●) and the outcome. Open on Pipeline Board → jumps to it on the board.
  • Conversations: the last WhatsApp message and a link to the full chat. Instagram and Facebook are marked "Soon".
  • Activity: Notes (the deal's notes) and Stage History. Tasks and Calls are marked "New" and aren't available yet.

✏️ Edit Lead has the same fields as Edit Deal, plus Assigned Salesperson. Type the name of the team member responsible. It's used on the Leads Overview team table.

Not yet qualified?If the page says "Awaiting qualification", the lead hasn't reached the Pipeline Board yet. Its ✅ Qualify and 🚫 Not a Fit buttons are explained in the Leads guide.

Each kind lives only in its own module. A WhatsApp Segment never appears in Email or CRM, and the other way round. So you can give someone Email Campaign without WhatsApp, and they'll see Email Segments only.

Rules that apply to all three
  • Private to whoever made it. Staff see only the segments they made. The business owner, and anyone whose role can manage the Team (for example an IT Admin), see every segment, with "Made by …" on each.
  • Same names are fine. Two staff members can each have a "Hot leads".
  • People, not deals. A segment holds contacts. Leads and deals are added through their contact, so nobody is in a segment twice.
  • When a staff member is switched off, their segments stay, marked "no longer active: now belongs to the business owner". Other staff still can't see them.
  • Deleting a segment never deletes the people in it.

CRM Segments

  • Each card shows the member count, name and description, and (for the owner) who made it.
  • View & Manage opens the segment. The pencil edits the name, description and colour. The bin deletes the list after you confirm.

Creating a CRM Segment

  1. Press New Segment.
  2. Type a Segment Name (required) and, if you like, a Description.
  3. Pick a Colour, then press Create Segment. It starts empty.

Adding and removing people

  • One at a time: in Add Contacts, the first 500 contacts are listed. Type a name, number or email and press Enter to search all your contacts, then press Add.
  • Many at once: on All Contacts, filter, tick the people and use Add to Segment.
  • From a profile: use the drop-down in the Segments box.
  • Remove: press ✕ next to a member and confirm.

Creating one

Type a name in New segment name at the top and press + Create. The segment opens, ready for people.

Email Segments

Email Campaign › Email Segment (or the 📋 Email Segments button on Email Campaigns)

Email Segments work exactly like WhatsApp Segments, with the same three sources, but they hold email addresses: only contacts with an email can be added, and the card shows how many can get email campaigns (people who haven't opted out of email).

  • In a new email campaign, choose Email Segment on the Recipients step. Contacts who opted out of email, and addresses that bounced, are left out. Exclude labels also leaves out anyone whose contact or deal carries that label (for example "Bounced").
  • Older groups. Email groups made before Email Segments existed (lists of leads) are listed under "Older groups". They still work in campaigns until you delete them with Delete.

Labels are short markers like VIP, Follow-up or Bounced. There is one shared list of labels, and you can put them on contacts and on deals.

One list everywhereLabels you add on a contact, on a deal, or here are the same labels. (They used to be called "Tags" on contacts.) The Customers label is added automatically when a deal is Won; you can also use it yourself.

Creating a label

Type a name in New label name and press + Create Label. A warning appears if a similar label already exists.

Import Bounces

📥 Import Bounces is for email campaigns. When emails "bounce" (the address doesn't exist), upload the bounce report here:

  1. Download the bounce report as CSV from your email sending service. It must have a "HARD BOUNCE" column and a "TO" column.
  2. Choose the file and press Import.
  3. The result shows how many hard bounces were in the file, how many matched a deal, and how many were newly labelled Bounced.

Those addresses are blocked from future email campaigns, which protects your sending reputation.

11Pipeline Settings

CRM › Pipeline Settings

Use your own words for the pipeline. Only the names change. The order, meaning and maths stay the same.

Pipeline Stage Names

There's a box for each of the six active stages and the three outcomes (Won, Lost, Dropped). The grey text in each box is the default name, and the meaning is written underneath. Type a new name only where you want one, leave the rest blank, and press 💾 Save Stage Names. Example: a property business might rename "Proposal Sent" to "Inspection Booked".

To go back to a default, clear the box and save.

Lead Score Labels

Rename the words Hot, Warm and Cold (for example "Ready", "Thinking", "Not yet"), then press 💾 Save Score Labels. The colours and the score ranges don't change: Hot is 60 and up, Warm 30 to 59, Cold under 30.

Staff who can view but not edit see "Your role doesn't allow changing them" and greyed-out boxes.

13Everyday tasks

Log a phone call with a customer

Open the contact (All Contacts › click the name), type what was said in the note box on the right ("Called, wants 40 units by Friday"), and press Add Note. If the call moved the deal forward, also move the deal on the Pipeline Board.

Import a list from a trade fair and message them on WhatsApp

  1. Put a whatsapp_number column and a labels column ("Trade fair Sept") in the spreadsheet. All Contacts › Import CSV.
  2. WhatsApp Campaigns › WhatsApp Segment › create "Trade fair Sept".
  3. All Contacts tab: search "Trade fair" or the names, and add them. (Or tick their deals on the Pipeline Board › 📱 Add to WhatsApp Segment.)
  4. Press 📣 Use on the segment to start the campaign.

Email everyone who has a proposal waiting

Email Campaign › Email Segment › create "Proposals out" › open it › CRM tab › tick Proposal Sent and Negotiating › Add everyone in the ticked stages › 📣 Use.

Mark everyone you messaged as Contacted

Pipeline Board › List › filter or search to find them › tick them › ➡️ Move Stage › Contacted › channel WhatsApp and today's date › Move Contacts.

Win a deal

Pipeline Board › ⋮ on the deal › Move to Won (from Negotiating) › enter the Won Date and final value › Confirm. The contact automatically gets the Customers label; their status isn't changed.

Someone asks you to stop sending offers

Open their contact › Consent › Opt out of all (or just the channel they mentioned). Don't delete them: the opt-out record is your proof.

Find contacts missing an email before an email campaign

All Contacts › Filters › Status: Customer, then compare with Has Email ticked. The difference is who needs an email added. Do the same with Has WhatsApp Number before a WhatsApp campaign.

Tidy two labels that mean the same thing

Labels › View / Edit on the one you'll remove › use Move to… to shift each person and deal onto the one you're keeping › then Delete the empty label.

Give the sales team a Monday update

Pipeline Overview › Last 7 days › 📄 PDF, and share the file.

14Questions and problems

I added a lead but can't find it on the Pipeline Board.

New leads wait on the Leads page until someone presses Qualify. Only then do they appear on the Pipeline Board.

The same customer appears twice in All Contacts.

Usually one has the number in the Phone number box and the other in the WhatsApp number box, or one number is missing its country code. Keep the one with the WhatsApp history, copy any missing details across with Edit, move their deals if needed, then delete the other. If a yellow "need a quick check" bar appears, use Merge Review.

My WhatsApp campaign count is lower than the number of people in the segment.

Campaigns only go to people with a WhatsApp number who haven't opted out. Someone whose number is only in the Phone number box won't receive it: edit them and put the number in the WhatsApp number box too, if it's on WhatsApp.

I can't see a segment my colleague made.

Segments are private to whoever made them. Ask them, or the business owner, who can see all segments.

I can't delete a contact.

They still have an open Sales Lead. Close it (Won, Lost or Dropped) or delete it on the Leads page first.

I changed the contact's status to Customer but the deal still shows Negotiating.

Status and deal stage are separate on purpose. Move the deal on the Pipeline Board.

Why is my good deal showing as Cold?

Probably nobody has touched it for over a week (−25), or the Sales Signals haven't been ticked. Open Edit Deal and tick what you know.

I can't move a deal backwards.

The ⋮ menu only moves forward. To move back, tick the deal in List view, use ➡️ Move Stage, and pick the earlier stage.

I closed a deal by mistake.

Closed deals can't be reopened. Create a new Sales Lead from the contact's page and add a note explaining why.

The import said "N skipped (no valid phone number, WhatsApp number or email)".

Those rows had none of the three, or only a number shorter than 7 digits. Fix them in the spreadsheet and import again. Rows already imported won't be duplicated.

The import was refused: "The first row of the file must name the columns".

Add a first row with column names, at least one of phone_number, whatsapp_number or email, and import again.

A menu item has a gold padlock.

Your plan doesn't include it. The owner can see plans under Settings › My Plan.

A button I've seen in this guide isn't on my screen.

Your role doesn't include it. Ask your team admin (see Who can do what).
