1How Social Media fits together

Write, design, plan, approve and publish posts for Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn and other networks, all from the portal.

Open Social Media in the side menu and you'll see these pages:

OverviewThis month at a glance: how many posts are at each stage, what needs someone now, and who is doing what.
New PostThe AI Post Designer. Describe a post or pick a product and the AI makes 4 designs plus a caption for each network.
Upload DesignPost a picture or video you made yourself, for example in Canva. No AI, nothing counted. Checks the size for each network.
CalendarEvery post on its day. Plan a post on any day, or let AI plan your whole month.
ContentEvery post that hasn't gone out yet: drafts, posts waiting for approval, scheduled posts and failed ones.
PublishedPosts that have gone out, with links to see them live.
AnalyticsHow each post performed: reach, likes, comments, clicks and more.
ApprovalSwitch approval on or off, and approve or send back posts that are waiting.
Brand KitYour logo, colours, business name and the voice the AI writes in.
AI Design UsageHow many AI designs you've used this month and how many are left.

How a post moves along

New Post, Upload Design or AI month plan→Draft→Awaiting approval (only if approval is on)→Scheduled→Posted
  1. You make a post with New Post (AI) or Upload Design (your own picture), or AI suggests a month of posts for you.
  2. You either send it now, schedule it for later, or save it as a draft to finish another time.
  3. If your business has approval switched on, team members submit posts and an approver checks them first. See section 10.
  4. Scheduled posts go to Buffer, which publishes them to your pages at the right time. Once Buffer confirms, the post moves to Published.
  5. A few days later the numbers (reach, likes and so on) show under Analytics.
What is Buffer?

Buffer is the service PhiXtra uses to publish to the social networks for you. You connect your Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn and other pages to Buffer once, then connect Buffer to PhiXtra (section 2). After that you do everything in PhiXtra; you don't need to open Buffer again.

Why you might not see Social Media, or a button

  • No Social Media in the side menu at all means Buffer isn't connected yet. The owner (or someone allowed) connects it under Integration › Buffer.
  • A gold padlock 🔒 next to a menu item means your business's plan doesn't include that page.
  • A button is missing means your role doesn't allow that action, for example only some staff may delete or approve posts. Section 15 lists every permission.

2Connecting your pages (Buffer)

Integration › Buffer. Usually done once, by the owner.

Before you start

You need a Buffer account (buffer.com) with your social pages already added inside Buffer. Add your Facebook Page, Instagram business account, LinkedIn page and any others in Buffer first.

Connecting

  1. Log in to Buffer and open Settings › API (publish.buffer.com/settings/api).
  2. Click Generate API key and copy it.
  3. In PhiXtra, open Integration › Buffer, paste the key into Buffer API key and press Check key & continue.
  4. If your key can see more than one Buffer organisation, press Use this organisation next to the right one.
  5. Under Choose where PhiXtra can post, switch off any account you don't want PhiXtra to post to, then press Save & finish.

The Social Media menu now appears. PhiXtra keeps the key locked (encrypted) and only ever shows its last 4 characters.

The connected page

ItemWhat it means
OrganisationThe Buffer organisation PhiXtra uses.
KeyOnly the last 4 characters are shown.
Connected byWho connected it and when.
Working / Needs attentionWorking means Buffer accepts the key. Needs attention means Buffer stopped accepting it (see below).
Check connection nowAsks Buffer again right away. Use it after adding a new page in Buffer so it appears here. PhiXtra also re-checks by itself every few minutes when you open Social Media pages.
Replace keyPaste a new key. The old one keeps working until Buffer accepts the new one.
DisconnectRemoves the connection. The Social Media menu disappears until you connect again.
Use in PhiXtraOne switch per social account. Switched off means that account isn't offered when making posts. Press Save after changing.
Reconnect in BufferShown next to an account that Buffer says needs reconnecting (for example a Facebook password change). Fix it inside Buffer.
"Buffer stopped accepting your saved key"

This usually means the key was deleted in Buffer or the Buffer account was closed. Posts can't go out until it's fixed. Create a new key in Buffer (Settings › API) and paste it under Replace key. The owner also gets one email when this happens.

Use the arrows at the top to look at another month, and This month to come back. Open calendar and + New Post are shortcuts.

Drafts with no date

A draft with no planned date isn't counted in any month. The page tells you how many there are; you'll find them at the bottom of the Calendar.

Needs attention now

Up to 5 posts in each group, from any month:

  • Awaiting approval: approvers see a Review link.
  • Overdue: past their due date and still not submitted or sent.
  • Sent back for changes: the writer needs to edit and resubmit.
  • Buffer didn't accept: failed posts to fix and try again.
  • Going out next: the next 5 scheduled posts.

The team table

One row per person responsible, with this month's posts split into Being worked on, Awaiting approval, Scheduled and Published, plus Overdue now. Click a name to see that person's posts under Content.

4New Post (AI Post Designer)

Social Media › New Post. Uses 1 AI design from your allowance each time the AI makes a set of designs.

Step 1: what the post is about

  1. What's this post about? Choose A product and pick one from your products (use Find a product to search), or choose An idea and describe the post in your own words, for example "We now deliver to Abuja".
  2. Anything to highlight? (optional) Add an offer or detail. Any price or number you type here is copied exactly. The AI never makes up prices.
  3. Where will it go? Tick the accounts. Each one gets a picture in the right size and its own caption.
  4. Picture, choose one (the first choice only appears when the post is about a product):
    • Use my product photo on brand colours (products only): your photo on a design in your Brand Kit colours.
    • Let AI create the picture: puts your product in a new AI scene, or makes a picture for your idea.
    • Upload my own image: JPG, PNG or WEBP, up to 15 MB.
  5. Press ✦ Create designs. The button tells you how many AI designs you have left. It can take up to a minute.
No AI designs left?

Press Use a ready-made layout (free) instead. You still get designs in your brand colours, just without new AI words and pictures.

Step 2: pick a design

You get 4 designs. Click one to select it. Then, all for free:

ControlWhat it does
StyleBold (big words, strong colour), Elegant (calm, lots of space), Playful (stickers and tilted labels) or Minimal (clean, product first).
Colours4 colour choices made from your Brand Kit.
LayoutPhoto right, Photo centre, Photo top or Bigger photo.
✎ Rewrite wordsShorter, More fun, More professional, Add urgency or In Pidgin. Changes the headline on the picture and all the captions together. 5 free rewrites per post.
👍 / 👎Tell the AI what you like. Future designs lean towards your taste.

When you're happy, press Use this design →.

Make new designs

None of the 4 right? Press None of these? Make new designs (uses 1 AI design).

  1. Tap everything that applies: Too busy, Wrong colours, Photo too small, Words too long, Doesn't look like my brand, Too plain, Want a different background.
  2. Add anything else in Anything else? (optional).
  3. Press ✦ Make 4 new designs. Your current designs are replaced. If the AI fails, it isn't counted.

Step 3: finish and send

Everything on this page is free.

  • Preview: the square picture (Instagram and square posts) and the wide picture (Facebook, LinkedIn, X).
  • Quick edits: change the Headline or Price label (leave it empty for no price), tick Show logo or Show contact line, change colours or layout, then press Update preview.
  • Captions: one box per account. Edit freely; a counter shows how long each one is.
  • Person responsible and Due date: see section 9.
  • When: Post now or Schedule for later (pick a date and time, in your own time zone).

Then press one of:

ButtonWhat happens
Post now / Schedule postSends it to Buffer. It shows as Scheduled under Content, then moves to Published once it's out.
Submit for approvalShown instead when approval is on and you can't approve. The post waits for an approver (section 10).
Save as draftKeeps it under Content to finish later. If you chose "Schedule for later", the draft keeps that date and appears on the Calendar.

5Upload Design

Social Media › Upload Design. For designs made in Canva or anywhere else. No AI, nothing counted.

Step 1: accounts and files

  1. Where will it go? Tick the accounts. Pinterest and YouTube can't be used here yet; they're shown greyed out with the reason.
  2. Upload your design: drag files in, paste (Ctrl / ⌘ + V), or press Choose files.
    • Images: JPG, PNG or WEBP, up to 20 MB each. Up to 10 images make a carousel.
    • Video: MP4 or MOV, up to 300 MB. Other formats (such as iPhone videos) are converted for you.
Exporting from Canva

Share › Download › PNG for designs with text or logos, JPG for photos, MP4 for video. Pick "Size ×1" or larger, never smaller. One design for everything? Use a 1:1 square, 1080 × 1080. It fits every network, with at most a light trim on X.

Step 2: the size check

Your files are checked against every network you picked:

ResultWhat it means
Fits perfectlyNothing to do.
May be croppedThe network may trim the edges. It never stops you posting. Press Fix for … only if you want to control what is trimmed.
Needs fixingToo small (under 1080 pixels wide) or a shape that network doesn't allow. That network is blocked until you fix it or untick it. Press Fix for ….

Also on this page: Remove or Replace a file, + Add more images (carousel), and Change accounts. How it will look shows a phone preview per network ("edges trimmed" marks where it's cut). Videos are processed in the background; the page updates by itself and you can leave and come back.

When everything is OK, press Continue to captions →. Start over throws the upload away.

Fixing a network

Only that network changes. Other networks keep what they have, and your original file is never changed. Choose:

  • Crop: pick the shape (the best one is marked), then drag the box to choose what stays. Some edges are lost.
  • Fit with borders: nothing is lost; the empty space is filled with your brand background, main or highlight colour, white, black, or a blurred copy of the picture.
  • Use a different version: upload another file just for this network, for example the same design resized in Canva.

Press Save for …. To go back, open the fix again and press Undo: use the original.

Step 3: captions and schedule

  • Carousel order: drag, or use ◀ ▶. The first image is the cover. If a network takes fewer images (X takes 4), only the first ones go there.
  • Caption: one caption for all networks, or tick Different caption per network. Each box shows that network's limit.
  • Person responsible, Due date and When: same as on New Post.

Press Post now / Schedule, Submit for approval or Save as draft. The post shows under Content with an Uploaded label.

Editing an uploaded post later

You can change its words, accounts and time. To change the picture or video, delete the post and upload again.

The size guide

Press Full size guide on the Upload page for every network's best shape and size in pixels, other shapes that work, the number of images allowed, video sizes and lengths, and the matching Canva preset name.

7Plan my month with AI

Social Media › Calendar › ✦ Plan my month with AI. Uses 1 AI design per plan, only if it works.

The AI suggests a month of posts: the day, the topic, a caption for each network, a call to action and a note on what the picture should show. You choose which to keep. Nothing goes on the calendar until you add it.

Filling in the form

BoxWhat to choose
MonthThis month or one of the next two.
How many posts4, 8, 12, 16 or 20.
Planned timeThe time of day every post is planned for.
Days of the weekPosts are spread evenly over these days, from tomorrow onwards.
Where will they go?The accounts. Each post gets its own caption for each one.

Press ✦ Suggest my posts. It can take up to a minute.

Better suggestions

The AI uses your Brand Kit voice, your About Us (Store Information), your products and your recent posts. If it knows little about your business, the page shows a tip. Add an About Us, add products, or describe what you do in "Anything to focus on".

Reviewing the suggestions

  1. Each suggestion shows its topic, type, the picture idea, the call to action and the captions.
  2. Untick any you don't want (or Untick all), and change the Day or Person responsible if you like.
  3. Press Add ticked posts as drafts. They go on the calendar as drafts with no picture yet. Each person gets one email listing their new posts.

Don't like the plan? Press Make a different plan. Old plans are listed under Plans already made on the form page. Adding a plan twice doesn't create doubles.

Making the picture for a planned post

  1. Open the draft (from the Calendar or Content). It shows the plan's topic and picture idea.
  2. Press Make the picture with AI or Upload the picture. The designer opens with the idea, accounts, person and due date filled in.
  3. On the last step, keep Put this into the planned draft ticked. The picture goes into that draft, and it keeps its day and person. Untick it to make a separate new post instead.

A post's page

Press Open on any post.

  • The picture, when it's planned for or goes out, the accounts, who made it and who approved it.

9Person responsible and due dates

On New Post, Upload Design, the AI month plan, and each post's page.

  • Person responsible: who is working on the post. You can pick the owner or any team member who can see Social Media. It starts as whoever made the post.
  • Due date (optional): the day the post should be ready. When you pick a "Schedule for later" time, a due date 2 days earlier is suggested; you can change it.
  • The new person gets an email when a post is given to them (not when you give it to yourself).
  • Overdue means the due date has passed and the post is still a draft (not submitted, not sent). Overdue posts are shown in red on the Calendar, Content and the Overview.
  • To change them later, open the post and use Person responsible and due date (drafts, scheduled and failed posts only). This doesn't change anything in Buffer.

10Approval

Social Media › Approval. An orange number next to Approval in the menu shows how many posts are waiting.

Switching approval on or off

The owner, or anyone with the approve tick, uses the Posts need approval before they go out switch. It's off until you switch it on.

  • Off: everyone who can post sends straight to Buffer.
  • On: team members who can't approve can only submit posts. The owner and approvers can approve, and their own posts go straight out.

Submitting a post (writers)

  1. Make the post as normal and pick the time you want it to go out.
  2. Press Submit for approval. It shows as Awaiting approval and approvers get an email.
  3. You get an email when it's approved or sent back.
  4. If it's sent back, open it, read the comment, press Edit and submit again, make the changes and submit.

Approving or sending back (approvers)

Each waiting post shows who submitted it, when it should go out, the accounts, the person responsible, the due date and the caption (See each version for per-network captions).

ChoiceWhat happens
At the planned timeGoes to Buffer for the time the writer chose. Not possible if that time has passed ("time has passed" is shown).
Post nowGoes out straight away.
Pick another timeChoose a new date and time.
✓ ApproveSends it with the choice above.
Request changesType what needs changing first (a comment is required). The writer sees it and gets it by email. The post shows as Changes requested.

Open full post shows everything, including the history. Decided in the last 14 days lists recent approvals and send-backs.

Team members who can't approve see this page too, with the posts they're waiting on.

11Published

Social Media › Published. Posts land here by themselves once Buffer confirms they went out.

  • Newest first, 50 per page (← Newer / Older →).
  • Tabs: All and Partly posted (went out on some accounts but not all). Filter by Person.
  • Went out: when it went out, in your own time zone.
  • Accounts: click an account name with ↗ to see the live post. An account marked "not posted" failed; the reason is shown.

  • Open shows the post's page; Delete removes it from PhiXtra only. It stays on the social networks.

12Analytics

Social Media › Analytics. The numbers come from Buffer, which collects them from each network.

Pick the month at the top. For each account you see how many posts went out, the totals for each number, and the average engagement rate. Below, a table shows each post's numbers, a ↗ link to the live post, and when Buffer last updated it.

What the numbers mean

NumberMeaning
ReachHow many different people saw it.
ImpressionsHow many times it was seen, counting repeat views.
ViewsVideo views.
Reactions / LikesLikes and other reactions.
CommentsComments.
SharesShares or reposts.
SavesTimes people saved it.
ClicksClicks on the post or its link.
Engagement rateReactions, comments, shares and clicks as a share of the people who saw it.
"Not given" or "n/a" isn't zero

It means that network doesn't send that number. For example, LinkedIn gives reach but not clicks, and Facebook gives clicks but not reach.

The numbers aren't live

Buffer updates them on its own schedule, often a few days after a post goes out. PhiXtra checks with Buffer when you open the page (at most every 6 hours). Press Get the latest numbers to check now. "Still waiting for Buffer's numbers" means Buffer hasn't sent any yet.

Only posts made in PhiXtra are counted.

14AI Design Usage

16Step-by-step recipes

Plan and prepare next month's posts

  1. Calendar › ✦ Plan my month with AI. Pick next month, 12 posts, Mon/Wed/Fri, your accounts, a person, and "Content due 2 days before".
  2. Review the suggestions, untick the weak ones, press Add ticked posts as drafts.
  3. Each person opens their drafts and presses Make the picture with AI or Upload the picture.
  4. Keep "Put this into the planned draft" ticked, choose Schedule for later (the day is already set) and send or submit.
  5. Watch Overview for overdue posts.

Post a Canva design today

  1. In Canva, download as PNG (or MP4 for video).
  2. Upload Design › tick your accounts › drop in the file.
  3. Fix anything marked "Needs fixing", then Continue to captions →.
  4. Write the caption, choose Post now, press Post now.
  5. Check Published a few minutes later for the live links.

Have a manager check every post first

  1. Owner: Team › Roles, tick "Approve or send back" on the manager's role only.
  2. Social Media › Approval, switch Posts need approval On.
  3. Staff make posts as normal and press Submit for approval.
  4. The manager gets an email, opens Approval, and approves or sends back with a comment.

Promote a product

  1. New Post › A product › find the product.
  2. Add the offer in "Anything to highlight?" (for example "10% off this weekend").
  3. Picture: Use my product photo on brand colours › ✦ Create designs.
  4. Pick a design, rewrite with Add urgency if you like, then finish and schedule.

See what worked last month

  1. Analytics › pick last month.
  2. Compare each account's average engagement rate, then sort the post table by eye for the posts with the most reach and comments.
  3. Make more posts like those. Use 👍 on designs like them so the AI learns.

17Questions and problems

There's no Social Media in my side menu.

Buffer isn't connected yet, or your role doesn't include Social Media. Ask the owner.

My post says Failed.

Buffer refused it. Read the red message under the post (often a missing image for Instagram, a caption that's too long, or an account that needs reconnecting in Buffer), fix it and press Try again.

A post says "Partly posted".

It went out on some accounts but not others. Published shows which account said "not posted" and why.

I can't see "Post now", only "Submit for approval".

Approval is on and your role can't approve. Submit it; an approver will send it out.

The approver can't choose "At the planned time".

That time has already passed. Choose Post now or pick another time.

An Instagram post won't send.

Instagram needs a picture or video. Text-only posts can't go to Instagram.

My analytics show n/a or nothing.

n/a means that network doesn't give that number. Nothing at all usually means Buffer hasn't sent the numbers yet; they come a few days after posting.

A post I made directly in Buffer isn't in PhiXtra.

Only posts made in PhiXtra are shown and counted.

I deleted a published post but it's still on Facebook.

Deleting in PhiXtra doesn't remove it from the networks. Delete it on the network itself.

It says I have no AI designs left.

This month's allowance is used. Use Upload Design or a ready-made layout (free), wait for next month, or buy extra AI designs.

A new page I added in Buffer isn't showing.

Integration › Buffer › Check connection now, then switch it on under "Use in PhiXtra" and press Save.

A button I've seen in this guide isn't on my screen.

Your role doesn't include it. Ask your team admin (see Who can do what).
