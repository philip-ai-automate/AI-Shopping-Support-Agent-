1How Leads fit together

A lead is someone who might buy. This module is where you decide which ones are worth your team's time.

Every enquiry starts life as a Lead. A lead waits on the All Leads page until someone decides what to do with it. There are only two choices:

  • Qualify it: "yes, this is a real chance of a sale". It becomes an Opportunity, leaves the Leads list and appears on your Sales Pipeline, where your team works it through to Won or Lost.
  • Mark it Not a Fit: "no, not worth chasing", with a reason. It closes and leaves the Leads list. It still counts in your reports.
Enquiry comes in→Lead (All Leads page)→Qualify→Opportunity (Sales Pipeline)→Won / Lost

Every lead is also linked to a contact in your CRM address book. If the person isn't in your contacts yet, the portal adds them for you. Qualifying or closing a lead never removes the contact.

Leads OverviewA management dashboard: how many leads, how fast they move, where they come from, how each team member is doing, and your sales results.
All LeadsEvery lead waiting for a decision. Add, search, filter, score, assign, Qualify or mark Not a Fit, one at a time or many at once.
How Lead Scoring WorksWhat makes a lead Hot, Warm or Cold, explained on one page.
Leads Report (in the Reports menu)New leads over time and which channels they came from, with CSV, Excel and PDF download.
Lead or Opportunity?

Same person, two moments. A Lead is "not decided yet". An Opportunity is "we're working on this sale". The Leads pages only show leads; the Sales Pipeline only shows opportunities. If you can't find someone on one, look on the other.

3The All Leads page

Side menu › Leads › All Leads

The page has two parts: Hot Conversations at the top (WhatsApp chats that sound like buying), and Your Leads underneath (the leads themselves). At the top right:

  • ⚙️ Settings opens Pipeline Settings, where you can rename the pipeline stages and the Hot/Warm/Cold names. See the CRM guide, section 11.
  • ➕ Add Lead opens the Add Lead form (section 5).

4Hot Conversations

All Leads › top of the page

The portal reads your WhatsApp chats and picks out the ones that sound like someone wants to buy: asking a price, checking stock, saying they want to order. Those chats show here as cards, Hot ones first.

Three counters sit above the cards: Hot — reply now, Warm — worth reading and Conversations flagged (the two added together). Each card shows:

  • the person's name, their last message (↙ from them, ↗ from you) and the buying words that were spotted;
  • a red or orange ribbon (🔥 Hot or ⭐ Warm) and a score ring out of 100;
  • Open chat → to go straight to the conversation in your Inbox;
  • + Create Lead to turn them into a lead in one click. If they're already a lead, you see ✓ Already a Lead instead, so you never make a duplicate.
Two different scores

The ring on a Hot Conversation card scores the chat (how much it sounds like buying). The Hot/Warm/Cold on a lead is a different score, built from your team's work on that lead (section 17). A hot chat can become a cold lead if nobody follows it up.

You only see Hot Conversations once a WhatsApp number is connected. Without one, you see "No WhatsApp connection" and a Connect WhatsApp → button.

6Importing a list (CSV or Excel)

All Leads › ⇪ Import (or CRM › All Contacts › Import)

Needs the ticks: "Import leads and contacts (CSV or Excel)" and "Create a Lead" (for Contacts: "Create / import a Contact")

Bring a whole list into the portal at once: a trade-fair list, a directory you bought, an old spreadsheet. Each business becomes a contact and a lead at stage New Lead, linked together. You can use the portal's template, or your own file with your own column names. Nothing is saved until the last step.

Preparing your file

  • Start from the template: ⤓ Download template (Excel) gives you a sheet called Your list with the column names already in row 1, and a sheet called How to fill it in. ⤓ Download template (CSV) has the same columns, for people without Excel. The Leads template has two extra columns: Deal value and Product / Service.
  • One business or person per row, with the column names in the first row.
  • Every row needs a Phone number, WhatsApp number or Email. Rows with none of the three are skipped. You can download them afterwards to fix them.
  • Numbers with the country code, starting with + (for example +234 803 123 4567 or +44 20 7946 0000). A number without it is still imported, but marked "⚠️ Check number". The portal never guesses a country code.
  • WhatsApp number only if it's on WhatsApp. The phone number is never copied into WhatsApp. Extra numbers go in Other numbers, separated by commas; they're saved in the lead's Notes.
  • Business category and Number of employees are free text: use the words your business uses, for example "Legal" or "6-10".
  • Labels: several in one cell, separated by commas.
  • The two grey example rows in the template (Example Foods Ltd and Example Legal Partners) are skipped by the import if you forget to delete them.
Already have a list?

Upload it as it is. Any column names work: you tell the portal what each column is in step 2. Excel files with several sheets are fine too; you pick which sheets to bring in.

The 4 steps

1. Upload. Choose the file (CSV or Excel .xlsx, up to 15 MB and 20,000 rows). For Excel, tick the sheets to import. Sheets whose columns don't look like a list (for example a summary or the instructions sheet) are left unticked. Sheets with the same column names are imported together.

2. Match columns. Each column in your file has a Save it as list, already filled in from its name. Check them and change any that are wrong. Don't import leaves a column out. You must match at least one of Phone number, WhatsApp number or Email, and the Company name (or Contact Person).

3. Options.

  • What should be created? Contacts and Leads, or Contacts only.
  • Add this label to every row: lets you find this whole import later with one filter.
  • Country: used for rows whose file has no country. Check it: it starts on your business's country.
  • Who should work these leads? Nobody yet, one person, or shared evenly between the people you tick (handed out in turn). Only people with the Assign tick see this. Staff who see only their own leads get the new leads themselves.

4. Check & import. Before anything is saved you see:

CountWhat happens
NewBecomes a contact and a lead.
Already an open leadSame phone, WhatsApp number or email as a lead you already have. Only its empty details are filled in; nothing is replaced, no second lead is made and it keeps its owner.
Repeat an earlier rowTwo rows in your file with the same number or email are joined into one. The later row fills the first one's empty details, and its extra numbers go into Notes.
SkippedNo phone, WhatsApp or email, or a template example row.
Check numberImported, but marked "⚠️ Check number" (no country code, or not a real number).
Have a WhatsApp numberHow many WhatsApp campaigns can reach.

Press Import. A progress bar shows while it saves; you can leave the page and the import carries on.

After the import

  • View imported leads / View imported contacts open the lists showing only this import.
  • Download skipped rows gives the skipped rows in your file's own columns, plus a Reason column, so you can fix them and import that file.
  • Download numbers to check lists every number marked "Check number".
  • Past imports on the Import page lists every import: when, which file, by whom, how many were added and skipped. Open one to download its files again.
  • Imported leads show Import as their Source.
Importing the same file twice

Rows already imported count as "Already an open lead" the second time, so nothing is added twice.

7Finding leads

All Leads › Your Leads

Search, Hot/Warm/Cold and sorting

  • Search by business or contact…: type and press Enter. It looks at the business name and the contact person's name (not phone numbers or email).
  • ☰ List / ▦ Board: switch between the table and the Hot/Warm/Cold board (section 8).
  • All, 🔥 Hot, 🌤️ Warm, ❄️ Cold: show only leads with that score. The number in brackets is how many there are.
  • 📊 Sort by Score: highest score first. Press again to go back to newest first.
  • Per page: 10, 25, 50, 100, 200, 500 or All. Page buttons (← Prev, 1 2 3…, Next →) appear underneath when there's more than one page.
  • 🎯 How this works: opens How Lead Scoring Works (section 17).

Filters

The second row has three filters. They work together and with the search and Hot/Warm/Cold buttons.

  • Contact details ▾: tick Has phone number, Has email, Has WhatsApp number, Has company and/or ⚠️ Numbers to check (the number in brackets is how many leads have one; see section 9), then Apply. Ticking two means "has both". The button shows how many are ticked, e.g. "Contact details (2)". Clear removes just these ticks.
  • Assigned to: Anyone, Assigned to me, Unassigned, or one person by name. It applies as soon as you choose.
  • Date added ▾: pick a From and/or To date, then Apply. The button then shows the dates you chose.
  • Clear all filters appears once any filter is on, and removes them all.
Your own to-do list

Salespeople: set Assigned to to Assigned to me and press 📊 Sort by Score. That's your leads, most promising first.

9Board view

All Leads › ▦ Board

The same leads, as cards in three columns: 🔥 Hot, 🌤️ Warm and ❄️ Cold. Each column shows how many leads it holds.

  • Each card shows the name, stage, deal value and contact person or phone. Click the name to open the lead.
  • Click the small score bubble (e.g. "● 70") to see why it has that score, signal by signal. Click again to hide it.
  • A long column shows the first cards and then "+6 more". Click it to see the rest in the List view.

Cards can't be dragged on this board, because a lead's column comes from its score, not from where you put it. The filters, tick boxes and bulk buttons are only in List view.

Edit Lead

Needs the tick: "Pipeline Board — Edit" (in the CRM part of Roles)

Press ✏️ Edit Lead to change any detail. The phone boxes work the same way as on Add Lead (section 5): saved numbers are shown with their + and country code. If a saved number still needs checking and you leave it exactly as it is, the lead still saves. Only a new or changed number has to pass the check. The window also holds the three Sales Signals that feed the score:

  • Budget confirmed: tick once they've told you what they can spend (+20).
  • Decision-maker engaged: tick once you're talking to the person who can say yes (+20).
  • Deadline / timeline stated: the date they need to decide by (+15).

Press Save Changes. Saving also counts as "touched in the last 48 hours" (+15), so a lead you've just worked on goes up straight away.

Activity, conversations and history

  • Activity (at the top, under the follow-up card): 💬 What happened lists every note written in Log/Follow-up, newest first (see What happened). 📝 Notes shows the lead's notes (change them with Edit Lead). ⏰ Follow-ups lists every follow-up date and whether it was done on time. 📞 Calls lists every call logged on this lead, with 📞 Log a call to log another (see Logging calls). 🕒 Stage History lists every change: when the lead was made, qualified, moved or closed, by whom and when. ✉️ Emails lists campaign emails sent to the lead.
  • Pipeline: for a waiting lead, "Not yet qualified — this Lead isn't in the Sales Pipeline yet" with the Qualify and Not a Fit buttons again. For an opportunity, a progress bar of the stages (section 16).
  • Conversations: the last WhatsApp message with this number and View full conversation →. Instagram and Facebook show "SOON" (not built yet).

⚠️ Numbers to check

Some numbers were saved before the country list existed: older imports, numbers typed as 0803…, or numbers that were given the wrong country code. The portal never guesses what they should be. It keeps them exactly as they were and marks them ⚠️ Check number when:

  • the number has no country code (it starts with 0),
  • it isn't a real number (wrong length, or no such number in that country), or
  • it's a premium-rate, free-phone or personal line, which is usually a mobile that was given the wrong country code.

For each one, open the lead and choose:

  • Fix it: press ✏️ Edit Lead, choose the right country next to the number (or retype it starting with +), and Save Changes. Only do this when you know the right country, for example after asking the customer.
  • Remove it: press Remove number under the number and confirm. Only that number is cleared; the lead and everything else on it stay.
WhatsApp campaigns skip these numbers

Until a number is fixed, WhatsApp campaigns don't send to it. The campaign report lists it as "Skipped — number to check". CRM › All Contacts has the same yellow note and filter for contacts.

11Follow-ups

A lead's page › Next follow-up · Side menu › CRM › Follow-ups · Sales Pipeline › ⋮ › Log/Follow-up

Every open lead has one next follow-up: a date, how you'll do it (Call, WhatsApp, Email, LinkedIn or Meeting) and what to do. Each time anyone contacts the lead, they record what happened and choose the next follow-up in the same step. Nothing gets forgotten, and a colleague can pick up any lead and carry on.

The follow-up card

The card sits at the top of every lead's page, under the name. It shows the date, a pill saying how far away it is, how to follow up, what to do, and the Last time box with what was said at the last contact.

  • The card turns amber on the day it's due and red once it's overdue. A lead with no date has a dashed border and says No date yet.
  • Log/Follow-up: the main button. Use it every time you contact the lead.
  • Change date (or Set a date on a lead with none): moves the date without logging a contact. See Changing the date.
  • A closed lead (Won, Lost or Not a Fit) has no follow-up, and the card says so.

Log/Follow-up: three steps

Press Log/Follow-up on the card. The same box also opens from 📞 Log a call on the Calls tab, Log a LinkedIn step, ↩️ They replied to our email, the Follow-ups page, and the ⋮ menu on the Sales Pipeline.

  1. What did you do? Called, WhatsApp, Email, LinkedIn or Meeting.
  2. How did it go? * Choose from the list. Each kind has its own answers (for a call: Answered, No answer, Busy / call back, Left a voicemail, Wrong number, Doesn't need us), and every list ends with Other. Then write What was said * (or What happened *). This can't be left empty: write enough that a colleague could carry on from here. For a call you can also add the minutes.
  3. Next follow-up: all three are required.
    • Date *: pick a day on the calendar, or press Tomorrow, In 3 working days or Next week. The dots under a day show how many follow-ups that salesperson already has then, so you can choose a quieter day. Past days can't be picked.
    • How will you follow up? *: nothing is chosen for you.
    • What to do *: one line, such as "Send the proposal for 3 branches".

Press Save and set next follow-up. The current follow-up is ticked off (on time or late), the next one is set, and the note is kept on the lead. If anything is missing, the line at the bottom of the box says what, and nothing is saved.

"Doesn't need us" closes the lead

Choosing Doesn't need us on a call, or Not a fit on a meeting, swaps step 3 for Close the lead: choose the reason and press Save and mark Not a Fit. No next date is needed. If your role can't use Not a Fit, the box asks for a next date instead and your manager sees the call in the Not a Fit Report.

What happened: the lead's history

Every note written in Log/Follow-up is kept in the 💬 What happened tab under Activity, newest first, with the kind of contact, how it went, who wrote it and when. It's the first tab once a lead has notes. The ⏰ Follow-ups tab lists every follow-up date the lead has had and whether it was done on time.

Changing the date

Use Change date only when you need to move the date without contacting the lead, for example when they've asked you to call after a holiday. If you did speak to them, use Log/Follow-up instead.

Every move is counted. Once the same follow-up has been moved 3 times, it shows Moved 3 times on the Follow-ups page, and Team performance flags the person as "rescheduled often".

The Follow-ups page

Side menu › CRM › Follow-ups

Each salesperson's work list. Start here every morning and work down it.

  • To do now: overdue first (red), then due today (amber).
  • Upcoming: the next 60 days, grouped by day.
  • No date set: open leads with no follow-up yet, mostly leads nobody has contacted since follow-ups started on 23 October 2026. See them all on Leads › opens All Leads with the Follow-up filter set to "No follow-up date".
  • Done: follow-ups ticked off in the last 7 days, each marked On time or how many days late.

Each row shows how and what to do, the date, and the last note. The buttons on the right let you work without opening the lead: a contact button that matches how you planned to follow up (Call, WhatsApp, Email or Open profile), Log/Follow-up, and Change date.

At the top right, On time this week shows how many of this week's follow-ups were done by their day. Staff see only their own follow-ups. People who can see all leads get Showing (Everyone, Just mine, one person, or Unassigned leads) and, under Everyone, a table with each person's overdue, due today, next 7 days, on time this week and open leads with no date. Click a name to see that person's list.

From the Sales Pipeline

On the Sales Pipeline, open a deal's ⋮ menu (List or Board) and press Log/Follow-up. The same box opens, and after saving you're back on the Pipeline.

Dates set for you, and reminders

  • A reply sent from the Inbox (WhatsApp, Messenger or web chat) ticks off the open follow-up and sets the next one 3 working days later, with no note. Change it from the lead if another day is better.
  • A demo outcome on the lead asks for the next date on the same calendar.
  • Won, Lost and Not a Fit cancel the follow-up. Reopening a lead starts a new one.
  • At 8am on working days each person gets their overdue and due-today follow-ups by email, WhatsApp and phone notification (using their alert settings). Each line says the business, how, what to do and when. Follow-ups still not done the next morning also go to the owner and the person's line manager.

12Qualify: turning a lead into an Opportunity

A lead's page › ✅ Qualify

Needs the tick: "Qualify a Lead into an Opportunity / mark it Not a Fit"

Qualify when you're confident there's a real chance of a sale: they have a need, a budget, and you can reach the person who decides.

  1. Open the lead and press ✅ Qualify.
  2. Check or fill in Contact Person and/or Company. You need at least one. If you type a company name that already exists in CRM › Companies, the lead is linked to it; if not, a new company is made. Capital letters don't matter, so you won't get duplicates.
  3. Press Qualify.

You'll see "Ibadan Bakery qualified — now an Opportunity on the Sales Pipeline." The badge changes to the first pipeline stage, the lead leaves the All Leads list, and it appears on the Sales Pipeline. The history shows "Qualified — became an Opportunity", with your name and the date.

13Not a Fit

A lead's page › 🚫 Not a Fit · Sales Pipeline › ⋮ › Not a Fit · a lead's page › 📞 Calls › Doesn't need us

Needs the tick: "Qualify a Lead into an Opportunity / mark it Not a Fit", or "Pipeline Board — Edit"

Not a Fit means "we're stopping: this business isn't right for us". Use it on any open lead, whether it's still waiting or already Qualified onto the Sales Pipeline, so nobody wastes another call on it. You can undo it later with Reopen.

The other way to close is Mark as Lost, on the Sales Pipeline only. It's for a Qualified deal where you tried and the customer said no (price, a competitor). Lost counts in your win rate; Not a Fit doesn't.

You must choose a reason:

  • Not our target customer
  • Too small
  • Doesn't use or need this type of service
  • No budget
  • Wrong product or service fit
  • Unresponsive
  • Wrong contact
  • Invalid lead
  • Duplicate
  • Other… (a box appears for you to type the reason)

Press Mark Not a Fit. The lead closes and leaves the Leads list (or the Sales Pipeline, if it was Qualified). Its page shows NOT A FIT and the reason. A Qualified deal also appears in Closed: Lost & Not a Fit at the bottom of the Sales Pipeline. The lead still counts in Leads Overview under "Marked not a fit" and in the Not a Fit Report, and the contact stays in your address book.

Check before you call

Before calling, look at the lead's Number of employees, Business category and Website. If the business is clearly too small or doesn't sell in a way that needs us, mark it Not a Fit without calling. That saves your time and theirs.

From a call: "Doesn't need us"

A lead's page › Log/Follow-up

When a call shows the business doesn't need you, close the lead in the same step as logging the call:

  1. Open the lead and press Log/Follow-up (or 📞 Log a call on the Calls tab).
  2. Choose Called, then under How did it go? choose Doesn't need us. Step 3 changes to Close the lead.
  3. The reason is set to "Doesn't use or need this type of service"; change it if another one fits better.
  4. Write what was said, then press Save and mark Not a Fit.

A meeting with the result Not a fit works the same way. If your role can't use Not a Fit, the box asks for a next follow-up date instead and the call is just logged. Your manager will see it in the Not a Fit Report. See the picture in Log/Follow-up.

Changed your mind? Reopen it

Needs the same tick: "Qualify a Lead into an Opportunity / mark it Not a Fit", or "Pipeline Board — Edit"

Open the lead and press ↩️ Reopen as Lead (at the top, or under the reason), then OK. The lead goes back on the All Leads list, waiting for a decision again, with its details, notes and assigned person unchanged. Its history shows "Reopened — back on the Leads list" and the old reason.

A Qualified deal shows ↩️ Reopen on Sales Pipeline instead. It goes back on the Sales Pipeline at the step it had reached. You can also press ↩️ Reopen on its row in Closed: Lost & Not a Fit on the Sales Pipeline.

Not a Fit or Lost

Not a Fit means you're stopping, because the business isn't right for you. You can use it on any open lead, including one already on the Sales Pipeline. Lost is only for a Qualified deal where the customer said no. See the CRM guide, section 7.

14Many leads at once

All Leads › List view › tick boxes

Tick boxes appear for anyone who can Qualify or Assign. Each button needs its own tick.

Ticking leads

Tick the box at the start of each row you want. A navy bar appears above the table showing how many you've ticked, with buttons ✓ Qualify, 👤 Assign to, Not a Fit and Clear (unticks everything).

The tick box in the table's heading ticks every lead on this page. If more leads match your filters than fit on one page, the bar offers Select all 15 matching leads. Click it to include all of them, even those on other pages. Undo goes back.

Filter first, then select all

To share out every unassigned Instagram lead from last week, set Assigned to › Unassigned and Date added, tick the heading box, then Select all … matching leads.

Qualify many

Press ✓ Qualify. A window shows what will happen before anything changes:

  • ✅ … will be qualified.
  • ⚠️ … skipped: no phone, email or WhatsApp number: a lead you can't contact won't be qualified. Add their details first. (This line only shows if there are any.)
  • 🏢 … have a company. It stays linked.
  • Assign to (optional, only with the Assign tick): Leave as they are, One person, or Share evenly between… (section 15).
  • Product / service interested in (optional): typed once, saved on all of them.

Press Qualify. A message tells you how many were qualified and skipped, and the page reloads. Unlike one-at-a-time Qualify, you don't need a contact person or company here. Each lead's history shows "Qualified (bulk)", who did it and when.

Not a Fit many

Press Not a Fit on the bar, choose one reason (it's saved on every lead you ticked), and press Mark Not a Fit. Their contacts stay in All Contacts.

Split test (email)

Needs the tick: "Edit sequences"

Shares the ticked leads evenly and at random between two email sequences, so you can see which email gets more replies. Press ⚖️ Split test on the bar, choose how many per group, the two sequences and a label for each group, and keep Balance the groups ticked. Leads with no email, unsubscribed or bounced, already in a sequence, or emailed in the last 30 days are left out, and the window tells you how many. The full steps are in the Email Campaigns guide, under Sequences › Split test.

15Giving leads to your team

All Leads, a lead's page, and the Sales Pipeline

Needs the tick: "Assign Leads and Opportunities to team members"

Each lead can have one Assigned Salesperson: you, or any active member of your team. There are four ways to set it:

  1. When adding a lead: Assigned Salesperson on the Add Lead form.
  2. On one lead: ✏️ Edit Lead › Assigned Salesperson.
  3. On many leads: tick them, then 👤 Assign to on the bar.
  4. While qualifying many: the Assign to part of the Qualify window.
  • One person: they all go to the person you pick. "(me)" marks you.
  • Share evenly between…: tick two or more people and the leads are dealt out in turn, like cards. The small number next to each name shows how many they'll get before you confirm.
  • Keep leads that already have someone (ticked by default, shown when some ticked leads are already assigned): leaves those alone and only shares out the unassigned ones. Untick it to reassign everything.

Press Assign. Each person then finds their leads with Assigned to › Assigned to me.

Good to know

Assigning doesn't change a lead's score. Whether staff see all leads or only the leads assigned to them depends on the "See all leads" tick on their role (see Who can do what). Staff who see all leads use the "Assigned to me" filter to focus on theirs. Your staff never see your (the owner's) name: where you are the assigned person, they see your company name instead. If someone leaves your team, their old leads show their name with "(no longer active)" in Edit Lead, so you can give them to someone else.

16After Qualify: the Sales Pipeline

Side menu › CRM › Pipeline Board

Once qualified, a lead is worked on the Sales Pipeline, through the stages Opportunity (or New Opportunity) › Contacted › Qualified › Proposal Sent › Negotiating › Won, or closed as Not a Fit or Lost. The CRM guide (sections 6 to 8) explains the Pipeline in full. Two buttons there belong with Leads:

  • 👤 Assign to: same as on the Leads page, for opportunities (needs the Assign tick).
  • ↩️ Move back to Leads: undoes a Qualify. The ticked deals leave the Pipeline and go back to the All Leads list, starting at the first stage again. Their history shows "Moved back to Leads". Closed deals (Won, Lost, Not a Fit) can't be moved back and are skipped. Needs the "Pipeline Board — Edit" tick.

17Lead score (Hot, Warm, Cold)

Side menu › Leads › How Lead Scoring Works

Every lead gets a score from six signals. The score decides its colour:

  • 🔥 Hot: 60 and up. Act now.
  • 🌤️ Warm: 30 to 59. Keep going.
  • ❄️ Cold: under 30. Needs attention or a decision.
SignalPointsHow it's set
Touched in the last 48 hours+15Automatic: someone saved a change to the lead in the last 2 days.
Reached Proposal Sent or later+25Automatic: the deal is at Proposal Sent, Negotiating or Won on the Pipeline. A lead that hasn't been qualified never gets these points.
Budget confirmed+20You tick it in Edit Lead.
Decision-maker engaged+20You tick it in Edit Lead.
Deadline / timeline stated+15You enter the date in Edit Lead.
No activity in 7+ days−25Automatic penalty when nobody has saved a change for over a week.

So a lead that nobody has touched for a week starts at −25, which is why most old leads are Cold. A waiting lead can reach 70 at most (15 + 20 + 20 + 15); the extra 25 for "Proposal Sent or later" only comes on the Pipeline.

Warming a lead up

Open the lead, look at the grey lines under Lead Score, and do what they say: confirm the budget, find the decision-maker, ask for a deadline. Then tick them in Edit Lead. Just saving a real update counts too.

To rename "Hot", "Warm" and "Cold" (for example to "Ready", "Interested", "Early"), use Pipeline Settings. The points and the 60 / 30 limits can't be changed.

18Leads Overview

Side menu › Leads › Leads Overview

A one-page summary for managers. Pick the period at the top right: the same choices as the Dashboard (Today, This week, This month and so on, or Custom… with your own dates and Apply). Arrows compare with the period before (▲ up, ▼ down).

  1. Leads at a glance: Total leads (all-time), New leads in the period, Not yet decided (waiting for Qualify or Not a Fit), and Average days waiting. Then Where your leads stand (Became an opportunity / Not yet decided / Marked not a fit, all-time) and a Hot, warm & cold ring for today.
  2. How fast leads move: of the leads added in the period, how many became opportunities and how many were marked not a fit, plus time to first contact and time to become an opportunity, in days.
  3. Where leads come from: Leads by source and Which source works best (the share of each source's leads that became opportunities).
  4. Team performance: each team member's leads given, opportunities, won and win rate. It fills in once leads are assigned. For each person's day, activity and targets, use Team performance (the See daily performance › link).
  5. Sales results: Win rate (won ÷ won + lost), Average sale amount, Average time to close a sale, and Revenue speed: roughly how much money your pipeline brings in per day (open opportunities × win rate × average sale ÷ average days to close).
"Not enough data yet"

When there aren't at least 3 records to work from, a tile says "Not enough data yet" instead of showing a misleading number. Filling in Source, Deal Value and Assigned Salesperson on your leads is what makes these tiles come alive.

19Team performance

Side menu › Leads › Team performance

Needs the tick: "Team performance" (Leads part of Roles). The owner always has it.

See how each salesperson is doing today and this month without asking them: who is working, what they did, what came out of it, and who needs a word. Every number comes from something the person did in the portal. The portal saves who did each action, and when.

Recording started on 11 October 2026

Days before that show "No earlier data yet" or "Not recorded", never a red zero, because nothing was saved then. Every working day from now on adds real numbers.

Who sees what

  • The owner sees everyone and the heading says Team performance · Whole company.
  • A staff member with the tick sees the people who report to them (and the people under those, all the way down), plus themselves. The heading says My team's performance.
  • Who reports to whom is the Line manager on each person in Team (Edit member). Change it there and the page follows straight away.
  • A person appears once they have open leads assigned to them, or did sales work in the period you're looking at. People with no leads and no sales work (for example a social media role) don't appear.

Reading the page

Pick Today, Yesterday, This week or This month at the top right. The days follow your business's time zone (the one in Settings, or your business country if none is chosen).

  1. Headline numbers: how many people logged in, Leads worked, Calls logged, New opportunities and Won (₦). Arrows compare with the day, week or month before.
  2. Needs your attention: things worth a word with someone. Red ones first.
    • Someone with leads hasn't logged in by 10:00 on a working day.
    • Someone has had no activity for 3 hours during the working day (09:00–18:00).
    • Someone is behind their month target (see Monthly targets).
    • Someone has assigned open leads that nobody has contacted since recording started.
    • A customer replied to an email more than a day ago and nobody has done anything on that lead since.
    Each line has a link: Open their day, See target, See leads (that person's leads on All Leads) or Open lead.
  3. The table: one row per person.

One person's day

Click a name. You see everything they did that day in time order, each linked to the lead, with any gap of an hour or more marked "No recorded activity for …". Use ‹ previous day, next day › or Go to a date. On the left: first and last time seen, active time, longest break, and the day's totals. See their leads › opens All Leads filtered to that person.

Gaps aren't always a problem

Lunch, a meeting, or a call made from a phone and not logged all show as gaps. Use the day view to ask the right question, not to judge.

Logging calls

A lead's page › Log/Follow-up

After calling a lead, open it and press Log/Follow-up (or 📞 Log a call on the Calls tab), choose Called, choose how it went, write what was said, add the minutes if you like, and set the next follow-up (see Log/Follow-up). The call shows on the lead with your name and the time, and counts on Team performance. Anyone who can open the lead can log a call on it.

A WhatsApp, email or meeting logged the same way also counts as contacted personally on Team performance, the same as a call.

Choosing Doesn't need us lets you close the lead as Not a Fit in the same step. See From a call: "Doesn't need us".

Monthly targets

At the bottom of the page, under Targets for (this month), type each person's target for New opportunities and Won (₦), then Save targets. Leave a box empty for no target. Targets are per month, so set them again at the start of each month. The owner can set everyone's; a manager can set the targets of the people under them, not their own.

On pace means they have at least as many opportunities as they should by today: the target × the share of this month's working days (Monday to Friday) that have passed.

The 6 pm email

Monday to Friday at 6:00 pm in your business's time zone, the portal emails a short summary: each person's day (first and last time active, leads worked, calls, replies, new opportunities, ₦ won), the warnings, the month so far against target, and an Open Team performance button. On Mondays it adds a line about last week. It also flags anyone who stopped working early, for example "had no activity after 13:20".

  • The owner gets the whole team.
  • Each line manager gets only the people who report to them, and only if their role has the Team performance tick.
  • Nobody gets it twice in one day.

The owner controls it at the bottom of the page, in Daily summary email: untick Send the daily summary email to stop it, untick a person so they stop getting it, then Save. Send me a preview now sends you a copy straight away.

How each number is counted

The portal saves these actions with the name of the person who did them: logging a call, a WhatsApp, Messenger or web chat reply, taking over a chat, adding a note to a contact or company, adding people to an email sequence (and each sequence email that then goes out), creating, editing or assigning a lead, Qualify, every stage move, Won, Lost, Not a Fit and Reopen, marking "They replied to our email", and everything logged with Log/Follow-up (calls, WhatsApp, emails, LinkedIn steps and meetings). Follow-ups add three numbers per person: follow-ups due, done on time, and missed, plus how many are overdue now and how many have been moved 3 or more times.

Not counted

Calls made outside the portal and not logged, emails sent from Outlook or Gmail directly, AI replies, and bulk imports. If your team works a lead outside the portal, ask them to log it on the lead (a call, or a note) so it counts.

Not a Fit Report

23Questions and problems

I qualified a lead and now I can't find it.

That's expected: qualified leads move to CRM › Pipeline Board. Search for them there. If it was a mistake, use Move back to Leads (section 16).

Why are almost all my leads Cold?

Any lead nobody has saved a change to in 7 days loses 25 points. Work the lead and record it (Edit Lead) and it goes up again. See section 17.

Why can't I see the tick boxes?

Your role needs either the Qualify tick or the Assign tick. Also, tick boxes are only in List view, not Board view.

Why does Assigned Salesperson say "— Not assigned —" and I can't change it?

Your role doesn't have the "Assign Leads and Opportunities to team members" tick. Ask the owner or your IT Admin.

The Qualify window says some leads were skipped.

Leads with no phone, email or WhatsApp number can't be qualified in bulk. Open them, add a way to reach them with Edit Lead, and try again.

Qualify one lead says "Enter a Contact Person and/or a Company".

One-at-a-time Qualify needs at least one of the two. Type the person's name or their company in the window.

I marked a lead Not a Fit by mistake.

Open it and press ↩️ Reopen as Lead (or ↩️ Reopen on Sales Pipeline for a Qualified deal). It goes straight back where it was (section 13).

Where has "Mark as Dropped" gone?

Not a Fit replaced it on 15 October 2026, so there are only two ways to close: Not a Fit (you're stopping) and Mark as Lost (a Qualified deal said no). Deals closed as Dropped before then still show as Dropped.

I logged "Doesn't need us" but the lead is still open.

Either the "Also mark … as Not a Fit" box was unticked, or your role can't use Not a Fit. Open the lead and press 🚫 Not a Fit, or ask your manager to.

Add Lead says the number "has no country code".

Choose the country in the list next to the number, or type the whole number starting with +, for example +234 803 123 4567. The portal never picks a country for you, not even your business's own country.

It says the number "doesn't look like a real number".

Check the digits and the country you chose. A Nigerian mobile typed with the UK chosen (or the other way round) is refused, because it isn't a real number in that country.

Why does a lead show ⚠️ Check number?

It was saved before the country list existed, or came from an import, and has no country code or isn't a real number. Fix it with Edit Lead or press Remove number (section 9, "Numbers to check").

Why did my WhatsApp campaign skip some people?

Numbers to check are skipped until they're fixed. The campaign report shows "Skipped — number to check" and the reason.

Can I delete a lead?

No. Use Not a Fit (reason "Duplicate" for a double entry). It closes the lead and keeps your reports honest, and it can be reopened if needed.

Does creating a lead message the customer?

No. Nothing on the Leads pages sends anything to the customer.

The same person shows up twice.

Hot Conversations and campaign replies check for an existing lead first, but Add Lead and Create Sales Lead always make a new one. Mark the extra one Not a Fit › Duplicate. Duplicate contacts are handled in CRM › Merge Review.

Leads Overview says "Not enough data yet".

There are fewer than 3 records for that number. Fill in Source, Deal Value and Assigned Salesperson on your leads, and record Won and Lost on the Pipeline.

Where do Instagram and Facebook leads come from?

For now, add them with Add Lead and choose Instagram or Facebook as the Source. Automatic Instagram and Facebook conversations on the lead's page are marked "SOON".

Team performance shows zeros for days before 11 October.

Recording of who-did-what started on 11 October 2026, so earlier days have nothing to show. They say "No earlier data yet" or "Not recorded".

My manager can't see me on Team performance.

Two things are needed: your Line manager in Team must be set to them, and their role needs the Team performance tick. You also need open leads assigned to you, or sales work in that period.

I made calls but Team performance says 0.

Only calls logged in the portal count. Open each lead › Log/Follow-up › Called.

Log/Follow-up won't save.

The line at the bottom of the box says what's missing. Everything marked * is required: how it went, what was said, and for the next follow-up the date, how, and what to do.

Where has Follow-ups gone from the Leads menu?

It's under CRM in the side menu, below Pipeline Board.

Why does a lead show "No date yet"?

Nobody has contacted it since follow-ups started on 23 October 2026. The first Log/Follow-up gives it a date. You can also press Set a date on the card to plan a first contact.

Someone shows "Idle" but says they were working.

Idle means nothing was recorded in the portal for a while. Work done outside the portal (phone calls not logged, emails from Outlook) doesn't show. Logging calls and notes on the lead fixes this.

How do I stop the 6 pm email?

Only the owner can: Leads › Team performance › Daily summary email at the bottom. Untick it for everyone, or just for one person, and press Save.
