1How Leads fit together

A lead is someone who might buy. This module is where you decide which ones are worth your team's time.

Every enquiry starts life as a Lead. A lead waits on the All Leads page until someone decides what to do with it. There are only two choices:

  • Qualify it: "yes, this is a real chance of a sale". It becomes an Opportunity, leaves the Leads list and appears on your Sales Pipeline, where your team works it through to Won or Lost.
  • Mark it Not a Fit: "no, not worth chasing", with a reason. It closes and leaves the Leads list. It still counts in your reports.
Enquiry comes in→Lead (All Leads page)→Qualify→Opportunity (Sales Pipeline)→Won / Lost

Every lead is also linked to a contact in your CRM address book. If the person isn't in your contacts yet, the portal adds them for you. Qualifying or closing a lead never removes the contact.

Leads OverviewA management dashboard: how many leads, how fast they move, where they come from, how each team member is doing, and your sales results.
All LeadsEvery lead waiting for a decision. Add, search, filter, score, assign, Qualify or mark Not a Fit, one at a time or many at once.
How Lead Scoring WorksWhat makes a lead Hot, Warm or Cold, explained on one page.
Leads Report (in the Reports menu)New leads over time and which channels they came from, with CSV, Excel and PDF download.
Lead or Opportunity?

Same person, two moments. A Lead is "not decided yet". An Opportunity is "we're working on this sale". The Leads pages only show leads; the Sales Pipeline only shows opportunities. If you can't find someone on one, look on the other.

3The All Leads page

Side menu › Leads › All Leads

The page has two parts: Hot Conversations at the top (WhatsApp chats that sound like buying), and Your Leads underneath (the leads themselves). At the top right:

  • ⚙️ Settings opens Pipeline Settings, where you can rename the pipeline stages and the Hot/Warm/Cold names. See the CRM guide, section 11.
  • ➕ Add Lead opens the Add Lead form (section 5).

4Hot Conversations

All Leads › top of the page

The portal reads your WhatsApp chats and picks out the ones that sound like someone wants to buy: asking a price, checking stock, saying they want to order. Those chats show here as cards, Hot ones first.

Three counters sit above the cards: Hot — reply now, Warm — worth reading and Conversations flagged (the two added together). Each card shows:

  • the person's name, their last message (↙ from them, ↗ from you) and the buying words that were spotted;
  • a red or orange ribbon (🔥 Hot or ⭐ Warm) and a score ring out of 100;
  • Open chat → to go straight to the conversation in your Inbox;
  • + Create Lead to turn them into a lead in one click. If they're already a lead, you see ✓ Already a Lead instead, so you never make a duplicate.
Two different scores

The ring on a Hot Conversation card scores the chat (how much it sounds like buying). The Hot/Warm/Cold on a lead is a different score, built from your team's work on that lead (section 15). A hot chat can become a cold lead if nobody follows it up.

You only see Hot Conversations once a WhatsApp number is connected. Without one, you see "No WhatsApp connection" and a Connect WhatsApp → button.

6Finding leads

All Leads › Your Leads

Search, Hot/Warm/Cold and sorting

  • Search by business or contact…: type and press Enter. It looks at the business name and the contact person's name (not phone numbers or email).
  • ☰ List / ▦ Board: switch between the table and the Hot/Warm/Cold board (section 8).
  • All, 🔥 Hot, 🌤️ Warm, ❄️ Cold: show only leads with that score. The number in brackets is how many there are.
  • 📊 Sort by Score: highest score first. Press again to go back to newest first.
  • Per page: 10, 25, 50, 100, 200, 500 or All. Page buttons (← Prev, 1 2 3…, Next →) appear underneath when there's more than one page.
  • 🎯 How this works: opens How Lead Scoring Works (section 15).

Filters

The second row has three filters. They work together and with the search and Hot/Warm/Cold buttons.

  • Contact details ▾: tick Has phone number, Has email, Has WhatsApp number, Has company and/or ⚠️ Numbers to check (the number in brackets is how many leads have one; see section 9), then Apply. Ticking two means "has both". The button shows how many are ticked, e.g. "Contact details (2)". Clear removes just these ticks.
  • Assigned to: Anyone, Assigned to me, Unassigned, or one person by name. It applies as soon as you choose.
  • Date added ▾: pick a From and/or To date, then Apply. The button then shows the dates you chose.
  • Clear all filters appears once any filter is on, and removes them all.
Your own to-do list

Salespeople: set Assigned to to Assigned to me and press 📊 Sort by Score. That's your leads, most promising first.

8Board view

All Leads › ▦ Board

The same leads, as cards in three columns: 🔥 Hot, 🌤️ Warm and ❄️ Cold. Each column shows how many leads it holds.

  • Each card shows the name, stage, deal value and contact person or phone. Click the name to open the lead.
  • Click the small score bubble (e.g. "● 70") to see why it has that score, signal by signal. Click again to hide it.
  • A long column shows the first cards and then "+6 more". Click it to see the rest in the List view.
  • + Add Lead at the bottom of each column opens the Add Lead form.

Cards can't be dragged on this board, because a lead's column comes from its score, not from where you put it. The filters, tick boxes and bulk buttons are only in List view.

Edit Lead

Needs the tick: "Pipeline Board — Edit" (in the CRM part of Roles)

Press ✏️ Edit Lead to change any detail. The phone boxes work the same way as on Add Lead (section 5): saved numbers are shown with their + and country code. If a saved number still needs checking and you leave it exactly as it is, the lead still saves. Only a new or changed number has to pass the check. The window also holds the three Sales Signals that feed the score:

  • Budget confirmed: tick once they've told you what they can spend (+20).
  • Decision-maker engaged: tick once you're talking to the person who can say yes (+20).
  • Deadline / timeline stated: the date they need to decide by (+15).

Press Save Changes. Saving also counts as "touched in the last 48 hours" (+15), so a lead you've just worked on goes up straight away.

Conversations, notes and history

  • Pipeline: for a waiting lead, "Not yet qualified — this Lead isn't in the Sales Pipeline yet" with the Qualify and Not a Fit buttons again. For an opportunity, a progress bar of the stages (section 14).
  • Conversations: the last WhatsApp message with this number and View full conversation →. Instagram and Facebook show "SOON" (not built yet).
  • Activity: 📝 Notes shows the lead's notes (change them with Edit Lead). 🕒 Stage History lists every change: when the lead was made, qualified, moved or closed, by whom and when. Tasks and Calls show "NEW" and aren't built yet.

⚠️ Numbers to check

Some numbers were saved before the country list existed: older imports, numbers typed as 0803…, or numbers that were given the wrong country code. The portal never guesses what they should be. It keeps them exactly as they were and marks them ⚠️ Check number when:

  • the number has no country code (it starts with 0),
  • it isn't a real number (wrong length, or no such number in that country), or
  • it's a premium-rate, free-phone or personal line, which is usually a mobile that was given the wrong country code.

For each one, open the lead and choose:

  • Fix it: press ✏️ Edit Lead, choose the right country next to the number (or retype it starting with +), and Save Changes. Only do this when you know the right country, for example after asking the customer.
  • Remove it: press Remove number under the number and confirm. Only that number is cleared; the lead and everything else on it stay.
WhatsApp campaigns skip these numbers

Until a number is fixed, WhatsApp campaigns don't send to it. The campaign report lists it as "Skipped — number to check". CRM › All Contacts has the same yellow note and filter for contacts.

10Qualify: turning a lead into an Opportunity

A lead's page › ✅ Qualify

Needs the tick: "Qualify a Lead into an Opportunity / mark it Not a Fit"

Qualify when you're confident there's a real chance of a sale: they have a need, a budget, and you can reach the person who decides.

  1. Open the lead and press ✅ Qualify.
  2. Check or fill in Contact Person and/or Company. You need at least one. If you type a company name that already exists in CRM › Companies, the lead is linked to it; if not, a new company is made. Capital letters don't matter, so you won't get duplicates.
  3. Press Qualify.

You'll see "Ibadan Bakery qualified — now an Opportunity on the Sales Pipeline." The badge changes to the first pipeline stage, the lead leaves the All Leads list, and it appears on the Sales Pipeline. The history shows "Qualified — became an Opportunity", with your name and the date.

11Not a Fit

A lead's page › 🚫 Not a Fit

Needs the tick: "Qualify a Lead into an Opportunity / mark it Not a Fit"

Use Not a Fit for leads that aren't worth chasing. You can undo it later with Reopen. You must choose a reason:

  • Not our target customer
  • No budget
  • Wrong product or service fit
  • Unresponsive
  • Duplicate
  • Other… (a box appears for you to type the reason)

Press Mark Not a Fit. The lead closes, leaves the Leads list and never reaches the Sales Pipeline. Its page shows NOT A FIT and the reason. It still counts in Leads Overview under "Marked not a fit", and the contact stays in your address book.

Changed your mind? Reopen it

Needs the same tick: "Qualify a Lead into an Opportunity / mark it Not a Fit"

Open the lead and press ↩️ Reopen as Lead (at the top, or under the reason), then OK. The lead goes back on the All Leads list, waiting for a decision again, with its details, notes and assigned person unchanged. Its history shows "Reopened — back on the Leads list" and the old reason.

Not a Fit, Lost and Dropped

Not a Fit is for a lead that never became an opportunity. Once something is on the Sales Pipeline, you close it as Lost (they chose someone else or said no) or Dropped (you stopped chasing it). See the CRM guide, section 7.

12Many leads at once

All Leads › List view › tick boxes

Tick boxes appear for anyone who can Qualify or Assign. Each button needs its own tick.

Ticking leads

Tick the box at the start of each row you want. A navy bar appears above the table showing how many you've ticked, with buttons ✓ Qualify, 👤 Assign to, Not a Fit and Clear (unticks everything).

The tick box in the table's heading ticks every lead on this page. If more leads match your filters than fit on one page, the bar offers Select all 15 matching leads. Click it to include all of them, even those on other pages. Undo goes back.

Filter first, then select all

To share out every unassigned Instagram lead from last week, set Assigned to › Unassigned and Date added, tick the heading box, then Select all … matching leads.

Qualify many

Press ✓ Qualify. A window shows what will happen before anything changes:

  • ✅ … will be qualified.
  • ⚠️ … skipped: no phone, email or WhatsApp number: a lead you can't contact won't be qualified. Add their details first. (This line only shows if there are any.)
  • 🏢 … have a company. It stays linked.
  • Assign to (optional, only with the Assign tick): Leave as they are, One person, or Share evenly between… (section 13).
  • Product / service interested in (optional): typed once, saved on all of them.

Press Qualify. A message tells you how many were qualified and skipped, and the page reloads. Unlike one-at-a-time Qualify, you don't need a contact person or company here. Each lead's history shows "Qualified (bulk)", who did it and when.

Not a Fit many

Press Not a Fit on the bar, choose one reason (it's saved on every lead you ticked), and press Mark Not a Fit. Their contacts stay in All Contacts.

13Giving leads to your team

All Leads, a lead's page, and the Sales Pipeline

Needs the tick: "Assign Leads and Opportunities to team members"

Each lead can have one Assigned Salesperson: you, or any active member of your team. There are four ways to set it:

  1. When adding a lead: Assigned Salesperson on the Add Lead form.
  2. On one lead: ✏️ Edit Lead › Assigned Salesperson.
  3. On many leads: tick them, then 👤 Assign to on the bar.
  4. While qualifying many: the Assign to part of the Qualify window.
  • One person: they all go to the person you pick. "(me)" marks you.
  • Share evenly between…: tick two or more people and the leads are dealt out in turn, like cards. The small number next to each name shows how many they'll get before you confirm.
  • Keep leads that already have someone (ticked by default, shown when some ticked leads are already assigned): leaves those alone and only shares out the unassigned ones. Untick it to reassign everything.

Press Assign. Each person then finds their leads with Assigned to › Assigned to me.

Good to know

Assigning doesn't change a lead's score. Staff can see all leads, not only their own; the "Assigned to me" filter is how they focus on theirs. Your staff never see your (the owner's) name: where you are the assigned person, they see your company name instead. If someone leaves your team, their old leads show their name with "(no longer active)" in Edit Lead, so you can give them to someone else.

14After Qualify: the Sales Pipeline

Side menu › CRM › Pipeline Board

Once qualified, a lead is worked on the Sales Pipeline, through the stages Opportunity (or New Opportunity) › Contacted › Qualified › Proposal Sent › Negotiating › Won, or closed as Lost or Dropped. The CRM guide (sections 6 to 8) explains the Pipeline in full. Two buttons there belong with Leads:

  • 👤 Assign to: same as on the Leads page, for opportunities (needs the Assign tick).
  • ↩️ Move back to Leads: undoes a Qualify. The ticked deals leave the Pipeline and go back to the All Leads list, starting at the first stage again. Their history shows "Moved back to Leads". Closed deals (Won, Lost, Dropped) can't be moved back and are skipped. Needs the "Pipeline Board — Edit" tick.

15Lead score (Hot, Warm, Cold)

Side menu › Leads › How Lead Scoring Works

Every lead gets a score from six signals. The score decides its colour:

  • 🔥 Hot: 60 and up. Act now.
  • 🌤️ Warm: 30 to 59. Keep going.
  • ❄️ Cold: under 30. Needs attention or a decision.
SignalPointsHow it's set
Touched in the last 48 hours+15Automatic: someone saved a change to the lead in the last 2 days.
Reached Proposal Sent or later+25Automatic: the deal is at Proposal Sent, Negotiating or Won on the Pipeline. A lead that hasn't been qualified never gets these points.
Budget confirmed+20You tick it in Edit Lead.
Decision-maker engaged+20You tick it in Edit Lead.
Deadline / timeline stated+15You enter the date in Edit Lead.
No activity in 7+ days−25Automatic penalty when nobody has saved a change for over a week.

So a lead that nobody has touched for a week starts at −25, which is why most old leads are Cold. A waiting lead can reach 70 at most (15 + 20 + 20 + 15); the extra 25 for "Proposal Sent or later" only comes on the Pipeline.

Warming a lead up

Open the lead, look at the grey lines under Lead Score, and do what they say: confirm the budget, find the decision-maker, ask for a deadline. Then tick them in Edit Lead. Just saving a real update counts too.

To rename "Hot", "Warm" and "Cold" (for example to "Ready", "Interested", "Early"), use Pipeline Settings. The points and the 60 / 30 limits can't be changed.

16Leads Overview

Side menu › Leads › Leads Overview

A one-page summary for managers. Pick the period at the top right: the same choices as the Dashboard (Today, This week, This month and so on, or Custom… with your own dates and Apply). Arrows compare with the period before (▲ up, ▼ down).

  1. Leads at a glance: Total leads (all-time), New leads in the period, Not yet decided (waiting for Qualify or Not a Fit), and Average days waiting. Then Where your leads stand (Became an opportunity / Not yet decided / Marked not a fit, all-time) and a Hot, warm & cold ring for today.
  2. How fast leads move: of the leads added in the period, how many became opportunities and how many were marked not a fit, plus time to first contact and time to become an opportunity, in days.
  3. Where leads come from: Leads by source and Which source works best (the share of each source's leads that became opportunities).
  4. Team performance: each team member's leads given, opportunities, won and win rate. It fills in once leads are assigned.
  5. Sales results: Win rate (won ÷ won + lost), Average sale amount, Average time to close a sale, and Revenue speed: roughly how much money your pipeline brings in per day (open opportunities × win rate × average sale ÷ average days to close).
"Not enough data yet"

When there aren't at least 3 records to work from, a tile says "Not enough data yet" instead of showing a misleading number. Filling in Source, Deal Value and Assigned Salesperson on your leads is what makes these tiles come alive.

17Leads Report

Side menu › Reports › Leads Report

Needs the tick: "Leads Sources" (in the Reports part of Roles)

  • Choose Last 7 days, Last 30 days or Last 90 days, or pick two dates and press Apply custom range.
  • New Leads: how many came in during that time.
  • New leads over time: a line, day by day.
  • Lead source: how many came from WhatsApp, Manual Entry, Facebook, Instagram, or Not recorded. "WhatsApp" includes leads made from a contact and approved campaign replies.
  • Export: 🧾 CSV, 📊 Excel or 📄 PDF download the report for the period you've chosen.

19Everyday tasks

Morning: share out yesterday's new leads

  1. All Leads › Assigned to › Unassigned.
  2. Date added ▾ › From and To = yesterday › Apply.
  3. Tick the heading box (and Select all … matching leads if offered).
  4. 👤 Assign to › Share evenly between… › tick your salespeople › Assign.

Salesperson: work your own leads

  1. All Leads › Assigned to › Assigned to me › 📊 Sort by Score.
  2. Open each lead, contact them, and record what you learn in ✏️ Edit Lead (notes, budget, decision-maker, deadline).
  3. When it's a real chance, ✅ Qualify. If not, 🚫 Not a Fit with the reason.

Turn a WhatsApp enquiry into a lead

  1. All Leads › Hot Conversations › Open chat → to read it.
  2. Back on All Leads, press + Create Lead on that card.
  3. Open the new lead (top of the list) and fill in the rest with Edit Lead.

Clean out old, dead leads

  1. All Leads › ❄️ Cold, and Date added ▾ › To = three months ago › Apply.
  2. Check the list, untick any you still want to chase.
  3. Not a Fit on the bar › Unresponsive › Mark Not a Fit.

Qualify a batch after a campaign

  1. All Leads › Contact details ▾ › tick Has WhatsApp number › Apply; filter by Date added to the campaign week.
  2. Tick the leads who replied with real interest (open them to check).
  3. ✓ Qualify › choose who gets them under Assign to › type the product › Qualify.

Fix the numbers to check

  1. All Leads › press Show them in the yellow note (or Contact details › Numbers to check › Apply).
  2. Open each lead. If you know the country, ✏️ Edit Lead › choose the country next to the number › Save Changes.
  3. If the number is wrong or you can't tell, press Remove number. Add the right one later with Edit Lead.

Undo a mistaken Qualify

  1. CRM › Pipeline Board › List view, find the deal.
  2. Tick it › ↩️ Move back to Leads › OK.

Monthly check on the team

  1. Leads › Leads Overview › period "This month" (or Custom…).
  2. Look at Team performance and Sales results; compare with the arrows for last month.
  3. For a board meeting, Reports › Leads Report › Last 30 days › 📄 PDF.

20Questions and problems

I qualified a lead and now I can't find it.

That's expected: qualified leads move to CRM › Pipeline Board. Search for them there. If it was a mistake, use Move back to Leads (section 14).

Why are almost all my leads Cold?

Any lead nobody has saved a change to in 7 days loses 25 points. Work the lead and record it (Edit Lead) and it goes up again. See section 15.

Why can't I see the tick boxes?

Your role needs either the Qualify tick or the Assign tick. Also, tick boxes are only in List view, not Board view.

Why does Assigned Salesperson say "— Not assigned —" and I can't change it?

Your role doesn't have the "Assign Leads and Opportunities to team members" tick. Ask the owner or your IT Admin.

The Qualify window says some leads were skipped.

Leads with no phone, email or WhatsApp number can't be qualified in bulk. Open them, add a way to reach them with Edit Lead, and try again.

Qualify one lead says "Enter a Contact Person and/or a Company".

One-at-a-time Qualify needs at least one of the two. Type the person's name or their company in the window.

I marked a lead Not a Fit by mistake.

Open it and press ↩️ Reopen as Lead. It goes straight back on your Leads list (section 11).

Add Lead says the number "has no country code".

Choose the country in the list next to the number, or type the whole number starting with +, for example +234 803 123 4567. The portal never picks a country for you, not even your business's own country.

It says the number "doesn't look like a real number".

Check the digits and the country you chose. A Nigerian mobile typed with the UK chosen (or the other way round) is refused, because it isn't a real number in that country.

Why does a lead show ⚠️ Check number?

It was saved before the country list existed, or came from an import, and has no country code or isn't a real number. Fix it with Edit Lead or press Remove number (section 9, "Numbers to check").

Why did my WhatsApp campaign skip some people?

Numbers to check are skipped until they're fixed. The campaign report shows "Skipped — number to check" and the reason.

Can I delete a lead?

No. Use Not a Fit (reason "Duplicate" for a double entry). It closes the lead and keeps your reports honest, and it can be reopened if needed.

Does creating a lead message the customer?

No. Nothing on the Leads pages sends anything to the customer.

The same person shows up twice.

Hot Conversations and campaign replies check for an existing lead first, but Add Lead and Create Sales Lead always make a new one. Mark the extra one Not a Fit › Duplicate. Duplicate contacts are handled in CRM › Merge Review.

Leads Overview says "Not enough data yet".

There are fewer than 3 records for that number. Fill in Source, Deal Value and Assigned Salesperson on your leads, and record Won and Lost on the Pipeline.

Where do Instagram and Facebook leads come from?

For now, add them with Add Lead and choose Instagram or Facebook as the Source. Automatic Instagram and Facebook conversations on the lead's page are marked "SOON".
