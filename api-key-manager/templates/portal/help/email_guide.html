1The big picture

Side menu › Email Campaigns

Email in PhiXtra works in three layers. You write an email once and keep it in Saved Emails. You then send it, either to a group in one go (a campaign) or as part of a planned run of emails a few days apart (a sequence). Every email sent is recorded on the lead, so the whole team can see who opened, clicked and replied.

1 · WriteNew Email, by hand or with ✨ AI
→
2 · SaveIt goes into Saved Emails
→
3 · SendIn a campaign (once) or a sequence (a run of emails)
→
4 · Follow upOpens, clicks and replies show on each lead

Where everything is

All CampaignsOne-off emails to a group, now or at a set time, and their reports.
New EmailWrite a new email and save it. Nothing is sent from here.
Saved EmailsYour library of written emails: preview, reuse, copy, share.
Email SegmentSaved groups of contacts to send to.
SequencesA set of emails sent days apart, which stops when someone replies.

Campaign, sequence or saved email?

You want to…UseWhy
Write an email you'll use againNew Email → Saved EmailsWrite once, reuse in any campaign or sequence.
Tell everyone about one thing (news, an offer, an event)CampaignOne email, one send, one report.
Reach new leads and follow up until they answerSequenceEmail 1 now, Email 2 a few days later, and so on. Each person stops getting emails as soon as they reply, unsubscribe or their lead is closed.
Follow up one lead you're working onThe lead's ✉️ Emails tabAdd them to a sequence, send their next email now, or mark that they replied.
Good to know

Your logo, colours, business address and an Unsubscribe link are added to every email automatically. Anyone who unsubscribes is never emailed again by your business, from any campaign or sequence.

2Writing an email

Email Campaigns › New Email (or + New email on Saved Emails)

The New Email page has the editor on the left and the real email on the right. It updates as you type. The bar at the top stays in view, so Save email is always one click away.

{{ shot('e02_new', 'The New Email page', 'New Email: the guide, the editor sections on the left, the live preview on the right.') }}
  1. Read the guide once. The 📘 How to use this page panel is open the first time. Hide it when you know the page. The button in the top bar brings it back.
  2. Write it, or let AI write it. See Write with AI.
  3. Fill in Email details, Message, Button and Sign-off, as described below.
  4. Check the preview and send yourself a test.
  5. Press Save email. You land on Saved Emails with your email in the list.
Careful

Nothing on this page is sent to customers. They only get the email when you use it in a campaign or a sequence. The only email that leaves this page is a test, and it goes to the address you type.

Subject lineUp to 120 characters. Phones show about the first 60, so put the important words first. Use Insert name ▾ next to it to add the person's name.

The message

{{ shot('e05_message', 'The message editor', 'Heading (optional) and the message editor with its toolbar.') }}
  • Heading is optional. Leave it empty for an email that looks personal, like one you'd write from your own mailbox. Sales emails usually work better that way.
  • Toolbar: text style, font, size, bold, italic, underline, colour, alignment, numbered and bulleted lists, ❝ quote, link, picture and clear formatting.
  • ❝ Quote puts a line in a coloured box. It works well for things customers ask, such as “Is this available?”.
  • Picture in the message: the picture button uploads a JPG or PNG where your cursor is.
  • Blank lines are tidied up automatically, so the email has even spacing.

Button, picture and video

{{ shot('e06_button', 'Button and Picture and video', 'One button with text and a link. Picture and video stay folded away until you open them.') }}
  • Button: give it both text that says what happens ("Book a demo") and a link starting with https://. Use one button per email so the next step is clear.
  • Picture above the message: optional. It sits at the top of the email.
  • Video link: email can't play video, so PhiXtra shows a picture with a ▶ play button that opens your link. YouTube links use the video's own picture; for other links you can upload one with Picture for the video. To place the video inside the message, type {{ '{{' }}Video}} on its own line. Otherwise it goes under the message.
  • Sign-off: the first line is your name, the lines under it are smaller. It starts as your business's default sign-off.

Preview

  • Preview as: pick a lead to see their names filled in, or "Someone with no name saved" to check the fallbacks.
  • Desktop / Phone: most people read email on a phone, so check both.
  • The line above the email shows what the inbox shows: your business name, the subject and the preview text.

Send a test

{{ shot('e09_test', 'Before you save checklist', 'The Before you save checklist turns green as you complete each item.') }}
  1. Under the preview, Send a test to starts with your own email address. Change it if you want the test somewhere else.
  2. Press Send test. The result appears right there, for example "✓ Test sent to you@… at 17:42 from sales@…".
  3. Open it on your phone and computer. Check the spam folder too.
Good to know
  • The test uses the names of the lead picked in Preview as, and the subject starts with [TEST].
  • You can send up to 20 tests an hour.
  • Sending a test needs the "Send an Email Campaign (incl. test sends)" tick on your role. Without it you'll see a note instead of the button.

Save, and edit later

  • Save email needs a name, a subject and a message. If something is missing, a message at the top says what.
  • The label next to the name shows where you are: Draft · not saved, Saved, or Unsaved changes. If you try to leave with unsaved changes, the browser asks first.
  • To change an email later, open Saved Emails › Use ▾ › Edit. Staff can edit only the emails they saved. To change someone else's, use Make a copy.
  • Changing an email that a sequence uses changes it for everyone who hasn't received it yet.

3Write with AI

New Email › ✨ Write with AI (top of the editor)

{{ shot('e03_ai', 'Write with AI', 'Type what the email is for, pick the type and tone, and press Write it.') }}
  1. What is this email for? Write a sentence or two: who it's for, what to mention, and what you want them to do. For example: "Follow-up for retail shops that didn't reply. Mention customers can send a photo on WhatsApp. Invite them to book a demo."
  2. Type of email: First contact, Follow-up, Offer, Newsletter, or Event / demo.
  3. Tone: Friendly, Professional, or Short.
  4. Press ✨ Write it. In about 5 to 20 seconds the AI fills in the subject, preview text, message and button words.
  5. Not quite right? Press ↻ Try again, Shorter, More formal or Friendlier. You can also just change the words yourself.
  6. Check the preview, add the button link if it's empty, and press Save email.
QuestionAnswer
What does it cost?Each new email uses 1 AI design from your plan's AI designs, the same allowance as the Social Media designer. Try again, Shorter, More formal and Friendlier are free, up to 5 per email. If it fails, nothing is counted.
What does the AI read?Your brief, your business name, your own website pages already in PhiXtra, and up to two of your saved emails as a style guide.
Can it make things up?It's told never to invent prices, discounts, dates, stock or results. As a safety net, any sentence with a number that isn't in your brief or your own information is removed automatically.
Will it send anything?No. It only fills in the editor. You decide what's saved and sent.
Where does the button link come from?It uses the link your saved emails use most often. If there isn't one, add it yourself; the box reminds you.
I can't see the AI boxYour role needs the "Write emails with AI" tick, and your plan needs AI designs. When none are left, the box says how to get more.

5One-off campaigns

Email Campaigns › All Campaigns

{{ shot('e16_campaigns', 'All Campaigns', 'All Campaigns: totals at the top, then every campaign with its status.') }}

A campaign sends one email to a group, now or at a time you set. The list shows each campaign's status, such as draft, scheduled, sending or sent. Any campaign that isn't sending right now can be edited or duplicated; drafts and scheduled ones can be deleted; sent ones have a report.

New Campaign, step by step

Press + New Campaign, or Use in a campaign on a saved email. The window has three steps.

Step 1 · Message

{{ shot('e17_compose1', 'Campaign step 1', 'Step 1: pick a saved email or write one, and give the campaign a name.') }}
  • Start from a saved template: fills in the subject and message from one of your saved emails.
  • Campaign name: for your team only.
  • Template Builder is the normal editor. Custom HTML is for people who already have a finished email design; it's sent exactly as written, with an unsubscribe line added.
  • Replies go to: the address that gets replies, which is yours by default.
  • 💾 Save as template saves what you've written into Saved Emails.

Step 2 · Recipients

{{ shot('e18_compose2', 'Campaign step 2', 'Step 2: who gets it.') }}
  • Sales Pipeline contacts: every lead you can see that has an email address.
  • Email Segment: one of your saved groups.
  • Paste email addresses: one per line.
  • Exclude label: tick labels to leave those people out.
  • People who unsubscribed are always skipped.

Step 3 · Schedule & Launch

{{ shot('e19_compose3', 'Campaign step 3', 'Step 3: send now or schedule, check the summary and preview, then launch.') }}

Choose Send Now or Schedule with a date and time. Check the review and preview, then press 🚀 Launch Campaign. Before launching, use ✉️ Send Test to send one to yourself.

Campaign reports

{{ shot('e25_report', 'A campaign report', 'A report: sent, failed, opened, clicked and replied, then each person.') }}
  • Opened is a guide only: some mail apps load pictures automatically, others block them.
  • Clicked counts a person pressing a link. Clicks in the first 90 seconds after sending are ignored, because company mail filters check links automatically.
  • Replied comes from the They replied button on a lead (see On each lead).

6Sequences

Email Campaigns › Sequences

A sequence is a set of emails sent a few days apart, such as a first email, then a follow-up three days later, then a final note a week after that. Each person moves through it on their own timetable, and stops getting emails as soon as they reply, unsubscribe, or their lead is closed.

{{ shot('e20_sequences', 'The Sequences list', 'Sequences: each one with its emails, whether it is running, and how many people are at each stage.') }}

Build a sequence

  1. On Sequences, type a name such as "Retail outreach" and press + New sequence. New sequences start paused, so nothing goes out until you start them.
  2. Under The emails, pick each email from your saved emails, then set:
    • Days to wait: Email 1 counts from when the person is added (0 = straight away); later emails count from the email before.
    • How it's sent: Send automatically goes out by itself when it's due. I'll send it myself waits in "Ready to send" for you to press Send, which suits emails you want to check or personalise first.
  3. Use + Add an email for more and ✕ to remove one. Check Replies go to, then press Save.
{{ shot('e21_seq_emails', 'The emails of a sequence', 'Four emails: three automatic, one sent by hand. Under each, its results so far.') }}

Once emails have gone out, each one shows Sent · Opened · Clicked · Replied and a See who link to its report.

Start and pause

{{ shot('e23b_seq_top', 'Sequence top bar', 'Running shows in green. Pause and Delete sit at the top right.') }}
  • ▶ Start sequence: automatic emails go out as they fall due. PhiXtra checks every minute, so an Email 1 with 0 days goes out within a minute or two.
  • ❚❚ Pause: nothing goes out automatically. You can still send anyone's next email yourself. When you start again, anything that fell due while paused goes out.
  • Delete removes the sequence and its people list. Emails already sent stay on each lead's history and in reports.

Changing a running sequence

  • You can change any email, its wait or how it's sent at any time. People who are part-way through carry on from the email number they reached.
  • Editing a saved email changes it for everyone who hasn't received it yet.
  • If you add an email to the end, people who already finished stay finished.

8A sales executive's routine

Every morning

  • Check your mailbox for replies. For each one, open the lead and press They replied, then answer them.
  • Open each running sequence and press the Ready to send filter. Read and send those emails, one at a time or with Send to everyone ready.
  • Look at Stopped: anyone stopped with "Email couldn't be delivered" needs a corrected address.

When new leads arrive

  • Add them to the right sequence, by business category or from the lead's page.
  • Check the "added / skipped" message. Skipped people are already in it, unsubscribed, or closed.

Every week

  • On each sequence, compare Opened, Clicked and Replied for each email. An email with far fewer replies than the others is the one to improve.
  • Improve it: Saved Emails › Use ▾ › Edit (your own) or Make a copy (someone else's), and try a new subject.
  • Follow up by phone or WhatsApp with leads that clicked but didn't reply. Their history shows the link they pressed.
