1How WhatsApp calls work

Customers press the call button in their WhatsApp chat with you. It rings in the portal for your team.

There is no phone line, SIM or extra app. When calls are switched on for one of your WhatsApp numbers, customers see a call button at the top of their WhatsApp chat with you. When they press it, the call rings in the portal, on the computer of every team member who is allowed to answer and can see that chat. It works like a shared phone line: the first person to press 📞 Answer takes the call, and everyone else sees who answered.

Call LogEvery call: answered, missed and made. Missed calls wait here until someone presses I'll call back.
Call SettingsOne page for each WhatsApp number: switch calls on, choose who it rings for, calling hours, the missed-call message, the call-back deadline and outgoing calls.
Calls ReportUnder Reports. How many calls came in, how many were answered, who answered, call-back times and the busiest hours.
The ringing pop-upShows on every portal page, bottom right, for people allowed to answer. Answer, mute and hang up from here.
📞 Customer calls→Their salesperson first (optional)→Everyone with access→Answered, or missed-call message

Everything is saved automatically: the call goes into the Call Log, a line goes into the WhatsApp chat in your Inbox, and if the caller is one of your leads, the call is added to that lead's history.

2Who can use calls

WhatsApp calls are part of the Enterprise plan.

  • Enterprise plans (Enterprise, Enterprise — Dual Agent, Enterprise — Website) include calls.
  • Businesses on a free trial get Enterprise features, so they can use calls during the trial.
  • On other plans, Calls (under Outreach) shows a 🔒 in the side menu and opens an upgrade page. Calls to your number are not put through; the customer gets your missed-call message instead.
Good to know

If you move to a lower plan, your call settings are kept. They work again as soon as you are back on Enterprise.

3Before you switch calls on

Five things to get right first, so no call is missed.

  1. Your WhatsApp number must be fully on PhiXtra. Meta doesn't allow calls in the portal on a number that also uses the WhatsApp Business phone app ("keep using the phone app"). Call Settings shows this as Connection.
  2. Meta's messaging limit. Meta allows calling on numbers that can message at least 2,000 people a day. If your number is below this, Meta refuses when you save, and the page tells you.
  3. Give your team the ticks. In Team › Roles, tick Answer WhatsApp calls for everyone who should answer (see section 11). They also need to be able to see the chat (Team › channel access).
  4. Keep the portal open. Calls ring in the browser. Someone with access must have the portal open in a browser tab (any page) during your calling hours. If nobody has it open, the call counts as missed.
  5. Allow sound and the microphone. Each person should click once anywhere on the portal page after opening it (browsers only play the ringtone after a click), and allow the microphone the first time they answer. A headset is best.

4Call Settings

Side menu › Outreach › Calls › Call Settings. Needs the Call settings tick.

Every WhatsApp number has its own settings. The page opens on your first number; pick another from the list on the left.

Numbers and checks

  • WhatsApp numbers (left). Each of your numbers with its flag and name. A green ● On means customers can call it; Off means they can't.
  • Status header. The number with Taking calls, Needs attention or Calls off; its name; Calling at Meta (On, Off or Not set up); and who last saved the settings, and when.
  • Four checks.
    CheckWhat it means
    ConnectionFully on PhiXtra is needed. Phone app number means the number also uses the WhatsApp Business phone app, and calls keep ringing on the phone instead.
    Messaging limit2,000+ a day is needed. Meta didn't say means Meta didn't share it; Meta tells you when you save if it's a problem.
    Calling customersWhether your team can call customers from this number. Not allowed here for Nigerian (+234), US and Canadian (+1), Egyptian (+20) and Vietnamese (+84) business numbers. Customers can still call you.
    Meta switchOn: Meta shows the call button to customers. It turns on when you save with calls switched on.

Incoming calls

The main switch. On shows the call button in customers' WhatsApp chat with you and rings your team in the portal. Off hides the button. A number whose settings have never been saved counts as off.

Who it rings for

A call rings for everyone with the Answer WhatsApp calls tick who can see that customer's chat. If the switch here is on, and the caller is a lead with a salesperson assigned, the call rings for that salesperson alone first, for the time you choose in Ring the assigned salesperson alone for (10, 15 or 20 seconds). Then it rings for everyone. The row of boxes under it shows the ringing order.

Calling hours

Switch on to take calls only at set times. Pick the Days (dark = on), the Opens and Closes times and the Time zone. The line underneath sums it up, for example "Calls go through Mon, Tue, Wed, Thu, Fri, 9am to 5pm." Outside these hours the call isn't put through and the customer gets your missed-call message. When it is off, calls go through any time.

Missed-call message

Sent on WhatsApp straight away to anyone whose call wasn't answered, or who called outside your hours. Type your own (up to 1,000 characters); the preview on the right shows how it looks on the customer's phone. Keep it short and say how they'll hear back.

Call-back deadline

How quickly a missed call must be called back: 15 minutes, 30 minutes, 1 hour or 2 hours (30 minutes to start with). The time only counts inside your calling hours, so a call missed just before closing is due soon after you open the next day. If nobody presses I'll call back in time, the business owner and everyone whose role has the Calls report tick get a phone notification and an email, once per missed call.

Outgoing calls

Adds a 📞 Call button to chats in the Inbox for people with the Call customers on WhatsApp tick. Not available on Nigerian, US, Canadian, Egyptian or Vietnamese business numbers (the switch is greyed out). See section 8.

Careful

Meta charges per minute for calls your team makes to customers.

Saving

As soon as you change anything, a bar appears at the bottom of the page.

  • Save and update Meta saves the settings and sends them to Meta (call button, calling hours, outgoing calls). You'll see "Call settings saved, and Meta has the new settings."
  • Discard throws your changes away.
  • If you try to leave the page with unsaved changes, your browser asks you first.
  • If Meta refuses, the page says why ("Meta refused the last change …") and calls stay off for that number until it's fixed.

5Answering a call

On any portal page. Needs the Answer WhatsApp calls tick.

When a customer calls, a pop-up appears at the bottom right of whatever portal page you're on, a ringtone plays and the browser tab flashes "📞 Incoming WhatsApp call".

ButtonWhat it does
📞 AnswerTakes the call. The first time, your browser asks to use the microphone: allow it. The pop-up changes to On a call with … with a timer.
Not meHides it for you only: "You passed. It keeps ringing for the others."
＋ Add as leadShown when the caller isn't in your Leads yet. Adds them as a new lead assigned to you (source WhatsApp, note "Added from a WhatsApp call"). Becomes ✓ Lead added ↗. Needs the Leads create tick.
Lead ↗Shown instead when the caller is already a lead. Opens the lead in a new tab.

The small line under the name shows "· your customer" when the call is ringing for you first because you're the lead's salesperson.

During the call

  • 🎙 Mute / 🎙 Unmute turns your microphone off and on.
  • Hang up ends the call. The pop-up shows "Call ended · Saved on the chat, the lead and the Calls page."
  • If you open that customer's chat in the Inbox, the chat header shows 📞 You're on a call.
  • You can only be on one call at a time. A second call shows "Finish your current call first."

When someone else answers

The pop-up stops ringing for everyone else and shows who took it. Anyone who opens that customer's chat sees 📞 On a call with … in the header.

Tip

If you've switched on phone notifications, your phone also gets "📞 name is calling on WhatsApp · Tap to answer. Ringing for your team." The call is answered in the portal.

6When nobody answers

What happens automatically, and what your team should do.

  1. The customer gets your missed-call message on WhatsApp straight away.
  2. The call goes into the Call Log under Missed · needs a call-back, with the reason and a countdown (⏱ Call back within 25 min, or ⏰ Overdue by 10 min once late).
  3. People who can answer get a phone notification "📵 Missed call from …" (not for calls outside hours or with calls switched off).
  4. One person presses I'll call back so everyone knows it's taken, then replies in the chat or calls back.
  5. If nobody presses it before the deadline, the managers get an alert: "⏰ Missed call not called back".
Reason shownWhat it means
Rang, nobody answeredIt rang in the portal but nobody pressed Answer before the customer hung up.
Nobody had the portal openNo one allowed to answer had the portal open, so nobody heard it.
Outside calling hoursThe call came outside the hours set in Call Settings. The Call Log shows it as Outside hours.
Calls switched off for this numberIncoming calls are off for that number in Call Settings (or your plan doesn't include calls). Shown as Calls off.

The reason line also says message sent or message not sent for the missed-call message.

8Calling a customer

Inbox › open the chat › chat header. Needs the Call customers on WhatsApp tick, and Outgoing calls switched on for a number Meta allows.

WhatsApp only lets a business call a customer who has agreed to it. The button in the chat header changes as you go:

  1. 📞 Ask to call sends WhatsApp's "Can we call you?" request into the chat. It becomes ✓ Asked.
  2. While you wait, it shows 📞 Asked (greyed out). Meta allows one request a day per customer.
  3. When the customer accepts, it becomes 📞 Call. Press it, allow the microphone, and the pop-up shows "Calling…" then "Ringing…". Mute and Hang up work the same as for incoming calls.
Good to know

Meta doesn't allow calling customers from business numbers in Nigeria (+234), the US and Canada (+1), Egypt (+20) or Vietnam (+84), so the button never shows for those numbers. Customers can still call you on them.

12Step-by-step recipes

Switch on calls for a number

  1. Open Outreach › Calls › Call Settings and pick the number on the left.
  2. Check the four boxes: Connection should say Fully on PhiXtra.
  3. Switch on Incoming calls.
  4. Set Calling hours if you want them, and check the Missed-call message.
  5. Press Save and update Meta. The header should change to Taking calls and the number on the left to ● On.

Get your sales team answering calls

  1. Team › Roles › edit their role › Calls: tick Answer WhatsApp calls and Calls page View. Save.
  2. Tick Calls report on the manager's role so they get overdue alerts.
  3. Ask each person to open the portal in their browser at the start of their shift, click once on the page, and keep the tab open.
  4. Do a test call from your own phone (below).

Test it with your own phone

  1. On a computer, open the portal and click once anywhere on the page.
  2. From your own phone, open WhatsApp, open the chat with your business number and press the call button.
  3. The pop-up should ring within a few seconds. Press 📞 Answer and allow the microphone.
  4. Talk for a few seconds both ways, try 🎙 Mute, then press Hang up.
  5. Check the Call Log shows Answered with the length, and the chat shows the call line.

Handle a missed call

  1. Open Outreach › Calls › Call Log › Missed · needs a call-back.
  2. Press I'll call back on the oldest one first.
  3. Press Open chat and reply, or call them back if outgoing calls are allowed on that number.
  4. If they're a new enquiry, press ＋ Add as lead.

Close for a holiday

  1. In Call Settings, switch off Incoming calls (customers won't see the call button), or untick that day in Calling hours.
  2. Change the Missed-call message to say when you're back.
  3. Save, and remember to switch it back after the holiday.

13Questions and problems

Customers don't see a call button.

Check Call Settings for that number: Incoming calls must be on and saved, and Meta switch must say On.

The call rang on my phone, not in the portal.

The number is still using the WhatsApp Business phone app. Connection shows Phone app number. Meta doesn't allow portal calls on these numbers.

No ringtone.

Click once anywhere on the portal page after opening it. Browsers block sound until you've clicked. Also check your computer isn't muted. The pop-up and the flashing tab still show either way.

"Your browser blocked the microphone."

Click the 🔒 or 🎙 icon next to the web address, allow the microphone for the portal, then press Answer again. While you fix it, the call keeps ringing for others.

"Answered by …" when I pressed Answer.

Someone pressed it a moment before you. Only one person can take a call.

"Meta didn't connect the call."

Meta refused to connect it. The call shows as Failed in the Call Log, with Meta's reason underneath.

A call shows "Calls off" but I switched calls on.

Calls are set separately for each number. Check the number the customer called (shown under the caller as "to …") is on.

Calls stopped coming in.

Check Call Settings for a red "Meta refused the last change" note, check calling hours, and check your plan still includes calls (Enterprise).

I don't see Calls under Outreach in the side menu.

Your role needs the Calls ticks in section 11. Ask the account owner.

Can calls be recorded?

No. Calls are not recorded. Write anything important as a note on the lead after the call.
