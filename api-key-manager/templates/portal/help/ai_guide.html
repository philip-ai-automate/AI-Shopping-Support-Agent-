Your AI follows a written instruction: who you are, what you sell and how to talk to customers. There's one for WhatsApp and one for the chat box on your website, each on its own page under AI Assistant in the side menu.

For owners and staff whose role can see the AI instructions.

1The two pages

Side menu › AI Assistant

💬 WhatsApp AI InstructionWhat your AI says to customers who message your WhatsApp number.
🌐 Website AI InstructionWhat your AI says to customers in the chat box on your website.

They are separate. Changing one never changes the other, so you can write each for the way customers use it. For example, on WhatsApp the AI can ask customers to reply ORDER, while on your website the product cards and order buttons do that job.

The page you're on says which chat it controls in the coloured box at the top: green for WhatsApp, blue for your website.

3WhatsApp AI Instruction

AI Assistant › WhatsApp AI Instruction

  • The green box shows which WhatsApp number this instruction is used on.
  • If you use AI agents, each WhatsApp number uses the agent chosen for it on WhatsApp Settings. Saving here updates that agent. With more than one agent, pick the one to edit under Which AI agent are you editing?
  • Manage agents opens AI Agent Profiles, where you add, rename or remove agents.
  • Press Save WhatsApp instruction (guided steps) or Save WhatsApp wording (your own wording). The next WhatsApp message uses it.

4Website AI Instruction

AI Assistant › Website AI Instruction

  • The blue box shows the website whose chat box uses this instruction.
  • You don't need to write about product cards, the hand-off form or the "talk to the team" button. PhiXtra adds those for you.
  • If you've never saved a website instruction, the chat box uses the standard PhiXtra instruction, and the blue box says so.

Copy from WhatsApp

  1. Press ⇣ Copy from WhatsApp. Your WhatsApp wording is placed in the wording box lower down the page.
  2. Change anything that only makes sense on WhatsApp, such as "reply ORDER".
  3. Press Save website wording.
Nothing is saved until you press Save

Copy from WhatsApp only fills the box. If you leave the page without saving, your website chat keeps its old wording.

5Guided steps or your own wording

Both pages give you two ways to set the instruction.

WayHowBest for
Guided stepsStep 1: pick what you sell. Step 2: tick what the AI should do. The preview shows the result. Then press Save.Getting started quickly.
Your own wordingOpen Edit full … instruction directly (advanced), change the text and press Save … wording.Your exact rules: brands you sell, delivery, payment, prices.
Pick one way

Saving the guided steps rebuilds the whole instruction, so anything you typed in the wording box is replaced. Once you've written your own wording, keep making changes in the wording box. If your wording is very different from the standard one, the page hides the guided steps for you.
