1How Team fits together

Give each member of staff their own login, and decide exactly what each person can see and do.

Your business has one account owner: the person who signed up. The owner can always see and do everything. Everyone else gets a team member login, and what they can do depends on the Role you give them.

Team MembersEveryone on your team, their role, department, location and status. Add, edit, reset passwords, deactivate or remove people, and choose which chats they see.
New Team MemberThe form for creating a new login.
RolesNamed sets of permissions, for example "Sales Rep" or "Support Agent". You tick exactly which pages and buttons the role can use.
DepartmentsYour own list of departments (Sales, Customer Support, IT…) to pick from when you add someone.
PositionsYour own list of job titles (Sales Executive, Support Officer…) to pick from when you add someone.
Make departments and positions→Make roles→Add team members→Share their password→Choose which chats they see
Who sees the Team menu

Ordinary staff never see Team at all. It only appears for the account owner, and for people whose role has Team ticks, for example an IT Admin. Your IT person (or the owner) decides who those "power users" are. Each person sees only the Team pages and buttons their own ticks allow.

2Team seats and your plan

Team Members page, the "Seats used on your plan" box.

Each active team member uses one seat. The account owner never uses a seat, and deactivated people don't use one either.

When every seat is used:

  • A red banner says "You've used all 3 team seats on your plan." with an Upgrade to add more → link.
  • The + New Team Member button turns grey and can't be pressed.
  • Re-activate is greyed out on deactivated people.

To free a seat, deactivate or remove someone, or upgrade your plan. On a plan with no seats, the banner says "Your current plan doesn't include team seats."

Create

  2. A green message appears at the top with their password: 12 letters and numbers, made up by the system.

  4. Send the email and password to the person yourself, for example by WhatsApp or in person.

5How your staff log in

portal.phixtra.com

  1. They open portal.phixtra.com and use the normal login page, the same one the owner uses.
  2. They type the email you entered and the password you gave them.
  3. They land on the Inbox.

Their side menu shows only what their role allows. For example, a Sales Rep with Inbox, CRM and Leads ticks sees just Dashboard, Inbox, CRM, Leads and Help & Tutorials, and no Team menu at all:

At the bottom of the menu they see their own name and the company name. Their settings page is called My Settings instead of Account Settings.

Good to know
  • Staff can't use "Forgot password". If they forget it, someone with the Reset Password tick makes a new one for them (section 10).
  • Deactivated people can't log in at all.
  • If you change someone's role, the change works from their very next click. They don't need to log out.
  • Help & Tutorials (including this guide) is open to every team member, whatever their role.

6Editing a team member's details

Team Members › Edit on the person's row

Fix a spelling mistake, move someone to a new department, give them a new title or line manager, or change their photo.

  1. Press Edit next to the person.
  2. Change any of: first name, last name, email, department, position, city, country, line manager, photo.
  3. Press Save Changes. You'll see "'Name' updated."
  • Email: this is their login. If you change it, tell them; their password stays the same. The new email must not already be used on PhiXtra.
  • Photo: choose a new file to replace it, or tick Remove this photo.
  • Line manager: you can't pick the person as their own manager.
  • Role, channel access and chat alerts are not on this page. Change those on the Team Members page. Editing never changes the password.

Needs the tick: "Edit a team member's details"

7Changing someone's role

Team Members › the drop-down in the Role column

  1. Click the role drop-down on the person's row.
  2. Pick the new role. It saves straight away, and you'll see "Role updated."

If you're not the owner, roles you're not allowed to give show as "(restricted)" and can't be picked (see section 13). If you don't have the tick to change roles, you just see the role name as plain text.

Needs the tick: "Change a team member's role"

9Chat alerts

Team Members › ⚙️ Manage channel access & chat alerts › 🔔 Chat alerts (also on the New Team Member form)

Tell a person straight away when a customer's chat needs a reply, so nobody is left waiting. They are only alerted about chats they can see (section 8).

  • Alert when a chat needs a reply: switches alerts on. They get a WhatsApp message and an email.
  • 10-minute reminder: one more alert if nobody has replied after 10 minutes. On by default.
  • Personal WhatsApp number: where WhatsApp alerts go. Use the full number with country code, for example +2348012345678 or +447700900123. A local number starting with 0 gets your business's country code added. Leave it empty for email alerts only.

Press Save. The label changes to On, and you'll see "Chat alerts switched on ✅" (or "email only" if there's no number). Alerts come from PhiXtra's WhatsApp number; emails go to the person's login email.

If the number is wrong you'll see "That WhatsApp number doesn't look right". Nothing is saved until you fix it.

Needs the tick: "Manage a team member's chat alerts"

10Reset Password

Team Members › Reset Password

Use this when someone forgot their password, never received it, or you think someone else knows it.

  1. Press Reset Password and confirm.
  2. Their old password stops working immediately.
  3. A green message shows the new password, once. Copy it and send it to them.

Needs the tick: "Reset a team member's password"

12Roles

Side menu › Team › Roles

A role is a named set of permissions. Make it once, then give it to as many people as you like. A person can do exactly what their role has ticked, nothing more. A role with nothing ticked can log in but sees nothing.

The Roles page

  • Features granted: how many ticks the role has.
  • Team members: how many active people have this role.
  • Edit opens the role; Delete removes it (see below).
  • + New Role makes a new one.

The first time a business opens Team, PhiXtra makes a ready-made Support Agent role with the 6 Inbox ticks. Change it or add your own.

Creating a role

  1. Press + New Role.
  2. Type a Role name, for example "Sales Rep". Each name can only be used once.
  3. Click a module (Inbox, CRM, Leads…) to open it, and tick what this role may do.
  4. Press Create Role in the bar at the bottom. You'll see "Role 'Sales Rep' created with 17 features granted."

The boxes at the top count the Modules, the Features available and how many are Selected for this role. The bar at the bottom shows the same count as you tick.

The tick grid

ColumnMeans
ViewCan open and look at the page.
CreateCan add new things there.
EditCan change existing things.
DeleteCan delete. These boxes turn red when ticked, as a warning.
OtherActions that aren't view/create/edit/delete, written out in words, for example "Reply to conversations", "Export Contacts (download CSV)".

An empty cell means that action doesn't exist for that row.

Helpful rules the grid follows for you

  • Ticking Create, Edit, Delete or an Other box also ticks View on that row, because none of them work if the person can't open the page.
  • Unticking View unticks the whole row.
  • The number next to each module (for example 7/25) shows how many of its boxes are ticked.
  • Select all on a module ticks everything in it; it then turns into Clear all.
  • Search permissions by name or key… shows only matching rows and opens their modules. The big Select all / Clear all buttons next to it only affect the rows you can see, so you can search "export", then Select all.
Plans and roles

Roles can only give what your plan includes. If a page has a 🔒 padlock in the side menu, it isn't on your plan, and ticking it in a role won't open it.

Editing and deleting a role

  • Edit opens the same grid. Modules that already have ticks open by themselves. Press Save Role. Everyone with this role gets the change from their next click.
  • Delete is greyed out while any active person has the role. Move them to a different role first (section 7), then delete it. Deleting can't be undone.

13The Super User rule (IT Admin)

Applies to every role that can manage other people

You can let someone else, usually your IT person, manage staff for you. To keep your money and data safe, PhiXtra puts limits on any role that can manage people.

The rule

If a role has any Team tick apart from "Team: view", then when it's saved PhiXtra automatically removes all Billing ticks and every Delete tick, even if they were ticked. The same happens to any role saved by someone who isn't the account owner. The yellow warning at the top of the role form reminds you.

What an IT Admin sees and can't do

  • No Billing module and no Delete boxes at all on the role form.
  • No Remove button on team members, and no Delete on roles, departments or positions.
  • They can only give people roles that have no Billing or Delete ticks. Other roles show as "(restricted)" in the drop-down and can't be picked.
  • They never see the account owner in the list.

Only the account owner can remove people, delete things, or give Billing access.

14Departments and Positions

Side menu › Team › Departments, and Team › Positions

Two simple lists you keep yourself. They appear as drop-downs when you add or edit a team member, and show on the Team Members list. They don't change what anyone can do; that's what Roles are for.

  • + New Department: type a name and press save. Each name can only be used once.
  • Edit: rename it. Everyone in it moves with the new name.
  • Delete: greyed out while any active person is in it. Move them first (section 6).

Positions work exactly the same way, for job titles.

16Step-by-step recipes

Set up a new sales rep

  1. Roles › + New Role, name it "Sales Rep". Tick Dashboard, the Inbox ticks, CRM Contacts (View, Create, Edit), Pipeline Board (View, Edit), and Leads. Press Create Role.
  2. Team Members › + New Team Member. Fill in their name and email, department "Sales", position "Sales Executive", role "Sales Rep". Press Create Team Member.
  3. Copy the password and send it to them with the link portal.phixtra.com.
  4. Open ⚙️ Manage channel access & chat alerts under their name and pick which chats they see, and switch on alerts if you want them.

Let your IT person manage staff

  1. Roles › + New Role, name it "IT Admin (Super User)".
  2. Open the Team module and tick what they should do: create and edit team members, change roles, deactivate, reset passwords, channel access, alerts, and create/edit roles, departments and positions. Add Dashboard and Inbox if they need them.
  3. Press Create Role. Billing and every Delete tick are removed automatically.
  4. Give your IT person that role.

Someone leaves the business

  1. Team Members › Deactivate on their row. They can't log in from that moment and their seat is free.
  2. Check the Inbox for any chats they were handling and pick them up.
  3. Later, when you're sure, the owner can press Remove.

A team member forgot their password

  1. Team Members › Reset Password on their row, and confirm.
  2. Copy the new password from the green message and send it to them.

Someone moves to a new department or gets a new title

  1. Team Members › Edit on their row.
  2. Change Department, Position and Line manager, then Save Changes.
  3. If their job changes what they should see, also change their role (section 7).

You need one more seat

  1. Deactivate someone who no longer needs access, or
  2. press Upgrade to add more → on the red banner to move to a bigger plan.

17Questions and problems

"+ New Team Member" is grey and won't click.

All your seats are used. Deactivate someone or upgrade (section 2).

"That email is already registered on PhiXtra."

That email is already used by someone on PhiXtra, maybe on another business. Use a different email, for example their work address.

I lost the password before I sent it.

Press Reset Password on their row to make a new one.

My staff member logs in but sees nothing.

Their role has nothing ticked, or not the pages they need. Edit the role and tick what they need. If the Inbox is empty, check their channel access (section 8): with several AI agents and none ticked, they see no WhatsApp chats.

The Role column says "No role, no access".

Their role was deleted while they were deactivated. Pick a role from the drop-down.

I can't delete a role, department or position.

Someone active is still using it. Move them first. If you're not the owner, you don't have Delete at all (section 13).

A role shows "(restricted)" and I can't pick it.

It has Billing or Delete ticks, and only the account owner can give those roles.

I ticked Billing or a Delete box and it disappeared after saving.

That role can manage people, so PhiXtra removed them on purpose (section 13).

Does a new team member get an email?

No. You send them the email and password yourself.

Do I need to ask them to log out after changing their role?

No. The change works from their next click.

They aren't getting chat alerts.

Check alerts are On, the WhatsApp number is full with country code, and that they can see the chat (channel access). Alerts only come for chats they can see.

A button I've seen in this guide isn't on my screen.

Your role doesn't include it. Ask the account owner or your IT admin.
