1What "selling currency" means

Your selling currency is the currency your customers see. The AI quotes prices in it on WhatsApp and in your website chat, and your deals, orders and reports in the portal use it. You can sell in one of three currencies:

₦1,250Nigerian naira
£1,250British pound
$1,250US dollar
Your PhiXtra plan is separate

What you pay PhiXtra for your plan doesn't change. You still pay in naira (₦) or pounds (£), and you choose which when you pick a plan. A business that sells in dollars can still pay for its plan in pounds.

2Choosing it when you sign up

Sign-up page › What currency do you sell in?

The sign-up page asks What currency do you sell in? for every kind of sign-up: WhatsApp only, Website only, or both.

  • If your business is in Nigeria, the UK or the US, the matching currency is picked for you when you choose your country. You can still pick a different one.
  • If your business is in any other country, nothing is picked. Choose the currency your customers pay in; you can't finish signing up without one.

3The one-time question on your dashboard

Dashboard › One quick question before you start

If you joined from the PhiXtra plugin inside WordPress or by chatting with PhiXtra on WhatsApp, you didn't see the sign-up page. The first time you log in, your dashboard asks the same question once.

  1. Pick the currency your customers pay in.
  2. Click Save. The box goes away and won't come back.

4Changing your currency later

Settings › Business Information › Selling currency

  1. Go to Settings and open Business Information.
  2. Choose your new currency under Selling currency.
  3. Click Save Business Information.

What happens when you change it

ThingWhat happens
New deals, orders and productsUse your new currency.
Deals, orders and products you already savedKeep their old currency, so a deal saved as ₦450,000 still shows ₦450,000.
Typing a new amount on an old deal or productThe new amount is saved in your new currency.
Product prices from your website or a price listConverted for your customers (see section 5).
Your own exchange ratesCleared, because they were written for the old currency. Enter them again if you use them.
Your AI instructionsThe standard pricing wording switches automatically. If you wrote your own wording, you get a reminder to check it (section 9).
Your PhiXtra plan priceNo change.
Only the owner can change it

Team members can't change the selling currency. Business Information is the account owner's page.

5Prices in another currency and exchange rates

Settings › Business Information › Some of your product prices are in another currency

Some product prices may be saved in a different currency from the one you sell in. A common example: your website keeps prices in pounds and has a currency switcher, but you sell to customers in dollars. PhiXtra converts those prices for your customers.

When this applies to you, a box appears under Selling currency. It lists each other currency your prices are in, and the rate used today.

Daily market rate (recommended)

The rate updates itself every morning, so you don't have to do anything. If the update ever fails, the last good rate is kept.

6Using your own exchange rate

If your website's currency switcher uses its own rate, use the same rate here so the prices on WhatsApp match your website exactly.

  1. In the exchange rate box, choose My own rate.
  2. Type your rate in Your own rate for each currency, for example 1.30 for £1 = $1.30.
  3. Click Save Business Information.
Leave a box empty to use the daily rate

With My own rate, any currency you leave empty still uses the daily rate. If you switch back to Daily market rate, your own rates are kept in case you need them again.

7What your customers see

Everywhere a customer sees a price, it's in your selling currency: AI replies on WhatsApp, the website chat, product lists and cards, discounts, order prices and payment reminders.

When a customer gives a budget without a currency sign, like "under 300", it's read in your selling currency.

Give it a minute

After you change your currency or rates, it can take up to a minute before every reply uses the new setting.

8Your deals, reports and totals

The Sales Pipeline, Leads, the Dashboard, reports and downloads, Team performance, Analytics, Orders, Customers, Companies and Products all show your selling currency.

Each deal keeps the currency it was saved in. After a change, older deals still show their old currency next to new ones:

Totals marked "≈"

Totals are always in your current currency. Older deals in another currency are converted at the daily rate (or your own rate) and added in, and the total shows ≈ to tell you it includes converted amounts.

9Your AI instructions

When you change currency, PhiXtra switches the standard pricing wording in your AI instructions for you. PhiXtra never changes words you wrote yourself.

10Questions and answers

Can I sell in euros or another currency?
Not yet. You can sell in naira, pounds or US dollars. Product prices saved in other currencies, such as euros, are still converted for your customers.
I don't see the exchange rate box. Why?
It only appears when some of your product prices are saved in a currency different from the one you sell in. If all your prices are in your selling currency, there's nothing to convert.
Why does a total show "≈"?
Because it includes deals or orders from before you changed currency, converted at the exchange rate. Each deal on its own still shows its exact saved amount.
Does changing my selling currency change what I pay PhiXtra?
No. Your plan is paid in naira or pounds, whatever you sell in.
Can my team change the currency?
No, only the account owner can, in Settings › Business Information.
I changed my currency but a reply still used the old one.
Changes can take up to a minute to reach every reply. Customers may also still see older replies higher up in the chat.
