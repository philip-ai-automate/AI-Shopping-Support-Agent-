1Connecting your Shopify store

Shopify admin › Apps › PhiXtra

You connect your store from inside Shopify, with the PhiXtra app. There's no plugin and no code to paste.

  1. Install the PhiXtra app in your Shopify store and open it.
  2. New to PhiXtra: choose who you sell to (Shoppers (Retail) or Businesses (B2B)), type your name and email, and press Create my PhiXtra account.
    Already use PhiXtra: press Link my account and use the email you log in to PhiXtra with.
  3. Type the 6-digit code we email you. It works for 10 minutes.
  4. You'll see "Your store is connected to PhiXtra ✓". PhiXtra starts reading your products straight away.

From then on, the portal knows your store is on Shopify and shows you the Shopify Store page instead of the WooCommerce pages.

2The Shopify Store page

Everything about your connected store in one place. It shows the same things as the PhiXtra app Home screen in Shopify, so you don't need to open Shopify to check them.

3Your products

Products & Orders › Shopify Store › 1. Products

PhiXtra reads the products that are on sale in your online store: names, prices, stock and pictures. Changes you make in Shopify reach your AI agent by themselves, and PhiXtra checks everything again each night.

  • Drafts, archived products and products hidden from your online store are left out, so the AI won't offer them.
  • To stop the AI offering one product, switch it off in Products & Orders › My Products & Services. It stays in Shopify.
  • Read products again reads everything from Shopify now. Use it if something looks out of date. It takes a few minutes.
No Excel list needed

Your products come from Shopify, so Add products from Excel isn't in your menu. If an Excel list was uploaded before your store was connected, the AI uses that list instead of your Shopify products: the Shopify Store page tells you, and Add products from Excel stays in your menu until you remove the list.

Password-protected store

While your store has a password (for example before you launch), the AI can read your products but not your store pages, such as delivery and returns. Remove the password in Shopify › Online Store › Preferences and the pages are read on the next check.

4The chat box on your store

Products & Orders › Shopify Store › 2. Chat box on your store

The chat box lets buyers talk to your AI agent on every page of your store. You switch it on once, in your Shopify theme:

  1. Press Switch on in your theme. Your Shopify theme editor opens with PhiXtra chat selected.
  2. Make sure PhiXtra chat is on, then press Save in the theme editor.
  3. Open your store. When the chat box has loaded, the page shows "Seen on your store" with the time.

If the chat box hasn't been seen for 7 days, the page shows a warning. It usually means it was switched off in the theme, or you changed themes.

5Business buyers (B2B plan)

Products & Orders › Shopify Store › 3. Business buyers

If you sell to businesses with Shopify B2B, PhiXtra reads your Shopify companies, their sites and their buyers. When a buyer chats, emails or messages you, PhiXtra matches them to their company, so the AI uses that company's prices and your salesperson sees the company on the lead and the quote request.

  • Changes you make to companies in Shopify are picked up by themselves.
  • Read buyers again reads all companies and buyers now.
  • When a buyer is matched only by email or phone (not logged in to your store), the AI doesn't name the company or share account details with them.

6From won quotation to Shopify order (B2B plan)

Quotations › Quote Won › a request

When a buyer accepts your quotation, you can turn it into an order in your Shopify, at the quoted prices.

  1. Open the won request. Under the buyer, press Create Shopify draft order. Every line of the quotation goes in at the quoted prices. Nothing goes to the buyer yet.
  2. Check the draft in Shopify if you like, then press Send Shopify invoice. The buyer gets an email with a secure link where they pay, or accept the payment terms of their company.
  3. When the buyer places the order, the status changes to Completed and Open the order in Shopify takes you to it.

The Shopify Store page lists these orders under Orders from your quotations: Draft order, Invoice sent or Order placed. Only orders PhiXtra made are shown. Your other Shopify orders stay in Shopify, and they don't appear on the Orders page in the portal either.

7What isn't used on Shopify

Some PhiXtra features are for WordPress and WooCommerce shops only, so a Shopify store doesn't see them in the menu:

  • WooCommerce Sync: your products come through the Shopify app instead.
  • API Keys: the Shopify app connects by itself, so there's no key to copy.
  • Order Messages: Shopify sends its own order emails.
  • Cart Recovery and the Cart Recovery Report.
  • Analytics: it's built from WooCommerce orders.

Everything else (Inbox, CRM, Leads, Quotations, Outreach, AI Assistant and Reports) works the same as for any other business.

9Disconnecting

To disconnect your store, uninstall PhiXtra in Shopify › Settings › Apps. PhiXtra stops reading your store straight away. 48 hours later, as Shopify requires, PhiXtra removes what it holds about your store, including your Shopify products on your AI agent. Your PhiXtra account stays, and you can connect a store again by installing the app.

10Questions and problems

A product is missing.
Check it's active and available on your Online Store in Shopify. Then press Read products again.
The page says the last product read didn't finish.
PhiXtra tries again each night. You can press Read products again now.
I don't see the chat box on my store.
Press Switch on in your theme, make sure PhiXtra chat is on and press Save. If you changed themes, switch it on in the new theme too.
My Shopify orders aren't on the Orders page.
That's expected. Shopify orders stay in Shopify. The portal shows only the orders PhiXtra made from your quotations, on the Shopify Store page.
I don't see Shopify Store in my menu.
Your role may not have the Shopify Store tick. Ask the person who manages your team.
