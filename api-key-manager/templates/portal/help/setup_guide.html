You don't need to prepare a product list. No Excel, no spreadsheet. If your website is on WordPress with WooCommerce, the free PhiXtra plugin sends your products in one click. On other websites, your AI can read your products from your own product pages. Uploading a list is only for businesses whose products aren't on their website.

2Step 1 on WordPress: install the PhiXtra plugin

If your website is built with WordPress (with or without WooCommerce), everything starts with the free PhiXtra – AI Sales Agent plugin. It comes from WordPress.org, the official place for WordPress plugins. The plugin does two jobs:

  • it puts the PhiXtra chat box on your website, with no code to copy and no key to paste;
  • on a WooCommerce shop, it sends your products to your AI in one click, and keeps them up to date after that.
You need to be an administrator of your WordPress website to add a plugin. If someone else looks after your website, send them this page. Websites hosted on WordPress.com can only add plugins on the WordPress.com plans that allow plugins.

A. Install the plugin from inside WordPress (easiest)

  1. Log in to your WordPress admin (usually yourwebsite.com/wp-admin).
  2. In the menu on the left, go to Plugins › Add New Plugin (called Add New in older WordPress).
  3. In the search box at the top right, type PhiXtra.
  4. Find PhiXtra – AI Sales Agent and click Install Now.
  5. When it has installed, the button changes to Activate. Click it.

B. Or download it from WordPress.org

  1. On your PhiXtra Dashboard, click Get the plugin from WordPress.org. The plugin's page opens.
  2. Click Download. A .zip file is saved to your computer. Don't open it.
  3. In your WordPress admin, go to Plugins › Add New Plugin › Upload Plugin.
  4. Click Choose File, pick the .zip file, then click Install Now.
  5. Click Activate Plugin.

C. Connect the plugin to PhiXtra

After you activate it, the plugin has its own menu in WordPress: PhiXtra AI.

  1. Open PhiXtra AI › PhiXtra AI Sales Agent and click Connect to PhiXtra.
  2. PhiXtra opens. Log in if asked, check the website address is yours, and click Connect yourshop.com.
  3. You're sent back to WordPress with the message "Connected to PhiXtra. The chat box is now on your website."

The approval link only works for the website on your PhiXtra account, only once, and only for 10 minutes. If it has expired, click Connect to PhiXtra again.

Step 1 ticks itself as soon as the plugin is connected. If you want to check straight away, click Check it's installed on your Dashboard.

D. Send your products: one click, nothing to prepare

The plugin doesn't send any products until you choose which ones, so you stay in control. On a WooCommerce shop:

  1. In WordPress, open PhiXtra AI › PhiXtra AI Sales Agent and click the Products tab.
  2. Under Which products can the AI recommend?, choose All published products. (Or choose only some categories, or only some products. See Products from your WooCommerce shop.)
  3. Click Save. That's it: your products, prices and stock go to your AI.

The top of the Products tab then shows how many products were sent and when. From now on, when you change a price, sell the last one, add, unpublish or delete a product in WooCommerce, the change is sent by itself. You never upload anything.

WordPress website without a shop? Skip part D. Your AI learns about your business from your website's pages in the next steps.

3Connect your website

Step 2 on WordPress. Step 1 on Wix, Shopify, Squarespace and other websites.

PhiXtra never contacts your website until you say yes. This step is where you say yes.

  1. Click Connect website in the Get set up box.
  2. Type your website address, for example yourshop.com.
  3. Tick the box: "This is my business's website (or I look after it), and I give PhiXtra permission to read its public pages so the AI can answer customers."
  4. Click Connect. PhiXtra checks it can reach your website. This takes up to 30 seconds.

When it works, you see Connection successful and how many pages PhiXtra found. Nothing has been read yet.

Connection failed? The message says why. Most often it's a firewall, Cloudflare or a security plugin stopping automatic visitors. The Website & Firewall guide shows how to let PhiXtra in.

4Sync your website

Step 3 on WordPress. Step 2 on Wix, Shopify, Squarespace and other websites.

Click Sync now, or Read my website now straight after connecting. Your AI reads your public pages: about, services, delivery, prices, policies, FAQs and contact. That's up to 100 pages, and it usually takes a few minutes.

  • What's skipped: menus, headers, footers, cookie notices, pages behind a login, and product pages. On WordPress your products come from the plugin; on other websites, choose Read products from my website.
  • See what was read: go to AI Assistant › Store Information › Sync Website. You can remove any page you don't want your AI to use.
  • Changed your website? Click Sync now again. On paid plans you can also choose to re-read it daily, weekly or monthly.

7Your products: nothing to prepare

Products are optional. If you add them, your AI can recommend them and answer questions about prices and stock. You don't type or prepare anything: they come from where they already are. They come from one of these places, never two, so the AI never sees two different prices for the same thing.

8Products from your WooCommerce shop

This is part D of step 1 on WordPress, in full. In WordPress, open PhiXtra AI › PhiXtra AI Sales Agent and click the Products tab. Choose one option, then click Save:

  • None: nothing is sent. This is where new shops start. Choosing None later removes products sent before.
  • Only products in these categories: tick the categories. Their subcategories are included.
  • Only these products: type a product name and pick it from the list.
  • All published products: what most shops choose.
  • Staying up to date: changes to the products you chose are sent by themselves, including price, stock, a sale that sells out the last one, unpublishing and deleting. Only published products are ever sent, and never customers or orders.
  • One product at a time: in WooCommerce › Products, each product has a Sync to PhiXtra link. It adds that product to your chosen products.
  • Checking: the top of the Products tab shows how many products were sent and when. Use Sync chosen products now after a big import or price change.

9Products from your website's pages

For websites without a shop plugin or a spreadsheet. Click Read products from my website on the Products card. Your website is read again, this time including product pages, up to 300 products.

  • Price, currency and stock are taken from the page's own shop information when it has it. Otherwise the AI uses the text on the page.
  • Prices update each time your website is read: click Sync now, or set a re-read schedule on a paid plan.
  • To stop, click Stop reading products. Those products are removed from your AI.

10Products from an uploaded list

Most businesses don't need this. On WordPress, use the plugin (one click). On other websites, read products from your website. Upload a list only if your products and prices aren't on your website.
  1. Go to Data Sources (the Upload a list instead link on the Products card takes you there).
  2. Upload your Excel or CSV file. The first row must be the column names. Use Download Sample Template if you need one.
  3. Match your columns: product name and price are needed; description, category, stock and image are optional.
  4. Click sync. Your AI can recommend the products straight away.
Products not in the list are removed. Each time you sync, any product that has gone from your list is taken off your AI. Keep your full product list in one file.

Removing the list on Data Sources takes its products off your AI.

11Adding WhatsApp

The WhatsApp card lets your AI answer on WhatsApp as well. If your plan includes WhatsApp, click Connect WhatsApp and use Meta's official sign-up window. You keep your number. If it doesn't, the card shows See Dual-Agent plans: Dual-Agent plans cover both your website and WhatsApp.
