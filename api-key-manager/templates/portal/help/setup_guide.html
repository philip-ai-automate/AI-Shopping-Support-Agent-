1The 4 steps

After you sign up, your Dashboard shows a Get set up box with 4 steps. Only the next step can be clicked. The ones after it are greyed out until the step before is done, so you always know what to do next.

1Connect your websiteType your address and give permission.
2Sync your websiteYour AI reads your public pages.
3Add the chat boxThe plugin on WordPress, one line of code elsewhere.
4Start your free trial14 days, no card needed.

Below the steps there are two Optional cards: Products and WhatsApp. You can finish set-up without them.

2Step 1: Connect your website

PhiXtra never contacts your website until you say yes. This step is where you say yes.

  1. Click Connect website in the Get set up box.
  2. Type your website address, for example yourshop.com.
  3. Tick the box: "This is my business's website (or I look after it), and I give PhiXtra permission to read its public pages so the AI can answer customers."
  4. Click Connect. PhiXtra checks it can reach your website. This takes up to 30 seconds.

When it works, you see Connection successful and how many pages PhiXtra found. Nothing has been read yet.

Connection failed? The message says why. Most often it's a firewall, Cloudflare or a security plugin stopping automatic visitors. The Website & Firewall guide shows how to let PhiXtra in.

3Step 2: Sync your website

Click Sync now in step 2, or Read my website now straight after connecting. Your AI reads your public pages: about, services, delivery, prices, policies, FAQs and contact. That's up to 100 pages, and it usually takes a few minutes.

  • What's skipped: menus, headers, footers, cookie notices, pages behind a login, and product pages, unless you choose Read products from my website.
  • See what was read: go to AI Assistant › Store Information › Sync Website. You can remove any page you don't want your AI to use.
  • Changed your website? Click Sync now again. On paid plans you can also choose to re-read it daily, weekly or monthly.

4Step 3 on WordPress: install the plugin

On WordPress and WooCommerce, the chat box comes from the free PhiXtra AI Sales Agent plugin. There's no code to copy and no key to paste.

  1. In your WordPress admin, go to Plugins, add a new plugin, and search for PhiXtra. Install PhiXtra – AI Sales Agent and click Activate. The Get the plugin button in step 3 opens the plugin's page on WordPress.org.
  2. In the plugin, click Connect to PhiXtra.
  3. PhiXtra opens. Log in if asked, check the website address is yours, and click Connect yourshop.com.
  4. You're sent back to WordPress with the message "Connected to PhiXtra. The chat box is now on your website."
Step 3 ticks itself as soon as the plugin is connected. No products are sent until you choose them. See Products from your WooCommerce shop.

The link only works for the website on your PhiXtra account, only once, and only for 10 minutes. If it has expired, click Connect to PhiXtra again.

5Step 3 on Wix, Shopify, Squarespace and other websites

On other websites the chat box is one line of code, made for your business. Step 3 shows everything you need:

  • your code, with a Copy code button
  • How to add it on …: the steps for your website builder
  • Send by email: sends the code and the steps to any email address, for example whoever looks after your website

How you know it worked

Open your website. A round chat button appears in the bottom corner, and clicking it opens the chat.

Step 3 ticks itself the first time the chat box loads on your website. PhiXtra doesn't need to visit your website for this. If you want to check straight away, click Check it's installed.

The code only works on your own website address, so it's safe to paste anywhere on your site or send by email.

6Step 4: Start your free trial

Click Start my free trial. You get every feature for 14 days, and no card is needed.

Before the trial, and on the free plan, the chat box still shows. Your team answers website chats from the Inbox instead of the AI.

7Products: one place at a time

Products are optional. If you add them, your AI can recommend them and answer questions about them. They come from one of these places, never two, so the AI never sees two different prices for the same thing.

Where fromBest forPrice and stock changes
Your WooCommerce shopWooCommerce shopsSent by themselves
An uploaded list (Excel or CSV)Products kept in a spreadsheetUpload or sync the list again
Your website's pagesWix, Shopify, Squarespace and custom sites with product pagesEach time your website is read

Switching: to switch to an uploaded list, tick "Yes, use my uploaded list instead" when you upload. The products from the old place are removed from your AI.

8Products from your WooCommerce shop

In WordPress, open PhiXtra AI › PhiXtra AI Sales Agent and click the Products tab. Choose one option, then click Save:

  • None: nothing is sent. This is where new shops start. Choosing None later removes products sent before.
  • Only products in these categories: tick the categories. Their subcategories are included.
  • Only these products: type a product name and pick it from the list.
  • All published products.
  • Staying up to date: changes to the products you chose are sent by themselves, including price, stock, a sale that sells out the last one, unpublishing and deleting. Only published products are ever sent, and never customers or orders.
  • One product at a time: in WooCommerce › Products, each product has a Sync to PhiXtra link. It adds that product to your chosen products.
  • Checking: the top of the Products tab shows how many products were sent and when. Use Sync chosen products now after a big import or price change.

9Products from an uploaded list

  1. Go to Data Sources (the Upload a list button on the Products card takes you there).
  2. Upload your Excel or CSV file. The first row must be the column names. Use Download Sample Template if you need one.
  3. Match your columns: product name and price are needed; description, category, stock and image are optional.
  4. Click sync. Your AI can recommend the products straight away.
Products not in the list are removed. Each time you sync, any product that has gone from your list is taken off your AI. Keep your full product list in one file.

Removing the list on Data Sources takes its products off your AI.

10Products from your website's pages

For websites without a shop plugin or a spreadsheet. Click Read products from my website on the Products card. Your website is read again, this time including product pages, up to 300 products.

  • Price, currency and stock are taken from the page's own shop information when it has it. Otherwise the AI uses the text on the page.
  • Prices update each time your website is read: click Sync now, or set a re-read schedule on a paid plan.
  • To stop, click Stop reading products. Those products are removed from your AI.

11Adding WhatsApp

The WhatsApp card lets your AI answer on WhatsApp as well. If your plan includes WhatsApp, click Connect WhatsApp and use Meta's official sign-up window. You keep your number. If it doesn't, the card shows See Dual-Agent plans: Dual-Agent plans cover both your website and WhatsApp.
