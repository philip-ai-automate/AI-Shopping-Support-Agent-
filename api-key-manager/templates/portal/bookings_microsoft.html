How it works
  • Sign in once with the Microsoft login that manages your Bookings page. PhiXtra can only read your bookings. It never books, changes or cancels anything.
  • Every 5 minutes it looks for new, moved and cancelled demos.
  • A new booking finds its lead by email, then by phone number. If nobody in your CRM has either, a new lead is made (source "Demo booking", unassigned).
  • The lead shows "📅 Demo booked" with the date, any email sequence they're in stops, and a New lead moves to Contacted.
  • Moved or cancelled on the Bookings page? The lead updates by itself within a few minutes.
  • After the demo time, the lead asks "Demo done?" so your pipeline stays accurate.
