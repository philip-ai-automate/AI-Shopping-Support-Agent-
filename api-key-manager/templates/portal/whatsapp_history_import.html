ℹ️How This Works
1
Export a chat from WhatsApp on your phone
Open the conversation → tap the contact name → Export Chat. Choose Include Media to keep photos/videos too, or Without Media for just the text (smaller file). WhatsApp creates a .zip (with media) or .txt (without) and lets you share it (e.g. email it to yourself).
2
Upload it below, one conversation per upload
Tell us which customer's number the file belongs to. This is manual and per-conversation — there's no bulk export from WhatsApp, so start with your most important recurring customers.
3
Text and media are both preserved
Message text, timestamps, and photos/videos/voice notes/documents (from a "With Media" export) all show up in the conversation thread. Anything WhatsApp itself marks "Media omitted" (deleted from the phone before export) can't be recovered — that's a WhatsApp limitation, not ours.
