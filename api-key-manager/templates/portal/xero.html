How it works
  1. A buyer accepts a quotation (online, by reply, or you mark the deal Won).
  2. On the deal or the quotation request, press Create Xero invoice.
  3. PhiXtra makes a draft invoice in Xero with the quoted lines and prices, for the buyer's Xero contact (found by email or name, or made new).
  4. Your accountant checks and approves it in Xero, and Xero sends it and chases payment as usual.
  5. The deal shows the invoice: awaiting payment, paid or overdue.
