How Lead Scoring Works

Every Lead in Sales Pipeline gets a score out of 100, built from six real signals on that Lead's record — not a guess, not from reading chat messages. The score decides whether a Lead shows as 🔴 Hot, 🟡 Warm, or ⚪ Cold, on both Sales Pipeline and the Leads page.

The 6 signals

Each signal adds (or subtracts) points. Add them up and you get the Lead's score. Green bars help the score, the red bar hurts it — the grey line down the middle is zero.

What each signal means, and how to log it

The first two read themselves automatically from the Lead's activity and Pipeline stage. The other four are things your team ticks or fills in on the Lead — there's no way to guess these from a WhatsApp message reliably, so they're logged by hand instead.

SignalPointsWhat it meansHow it's set
Touched in the last 48 hours +15 Someone updated this Lead — any edit — in the last 2 days. Automatic — based on when the Lead was last saved.
Reached Proposal Sent or later +25 The deal has made real progress — sitting at Proposal Sent, Negotiating, or Won. Automatic — based on the Lead's current Pipeline stage.
Budget confirmed +20 The customer has told you what they're able/willing to spend. Tick the "Budget confirmed" box on the Lead once you know this.
Decision-maker engaged +20 You're talking to the person who can actually say yes — not just a gatekeeper. Tick the "Decision-maker engaged" box on the Lead once you know this.
Deadline / timeline stated +15 The customer has given you a real date — "needs to decide by end of month." Enter that date in the Lead's "Deadline" field.
No activity in 7+ days −25 Nobody has touched this Lead in over a week — it's going cold. Automatic — this is a penalty, not something to tick.
