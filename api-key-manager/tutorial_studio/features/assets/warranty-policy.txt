Warranty Policy — Zemich Boutique

All laptops and mobile phones sold by Zemich Boutique carry a minimum
3-month warranty covering manufacturing defects and hardware faults.

What is covered:
- Faulty screens, batteries, and charging ports
- Devices that fail to power on under normal use
- Manufacturing defects present at the time of purchase

What is not covered:
- Accidental damage, water damage, or physical impact
- Software issues caused by third-party apps
- Normal wear and tear after extended use

To claim a warranty repair or replacement, contact us with your order
number and a description of the fault. Approved claims are repaired or
replaced within 5-7 business days.
